PHASE 4 — Add Market Risk Filter

Before any trade recommendation, feedback, or AI coach suggestion, the system should check market conditions.

Create risk labels:

- SAFE
- CAUTION
- DANGER
- NO TRADE

Rules:

- High volatility + wide spread = DANGER
- Low liquidity = CAUTION or NO TRADE
- Choppy market = CAUTION
- Clean trend + good liquidity = SAFE
- Major reversal risk = DANGER
- Spread too wide = NO TRADE

PHASE 7 — Connect to AI Coach

Update AI Coach so it references market conditions.
