Now build Phase 11: Advanced Trade Management.

Goal:
Once a trade is open, the app should not just forget it. It should monitor, manage, and protect the trade.

Create folders:

/server/trade-management
/server/trade-management/rules
/server/trade-management/monitoring

Create files:

1. /server/trade-management/tradeManager.ts

Functions:
- monitorOpenTrades()
- evaluateTradeHealth()
- suggestStopMove()
- suggestPartialClose()
- suggestEarlyExit()
- suggestTrailStop()
- detectInvalidation()

2. /server/trade-management/rules/breakEvenRules.ts

Move stop to break-even only when:
- price reaches 1R
- market structure still supports trade
- spread is normal
- no major news risk
- trade is not too new

3. /server/trade-management/rules/trailingStopRules.ts

Trail stop based on:
- ATR
- swing lows for buys
- swing highs for sells
- EMA 20 for trending trades

Do not trail too tightly.

4. /server/trade-management/rules/partialCloseRules.ts

Suggest partial close when:
- price reaches 1.5R or 2R
- market shows rejection near major level
- volatility spikes against position
- news risk approaches

5. /server/trade-management/rules/earlyExitRules.ts

Suggest exit when:
- trade thesis invalidated
- opposite break of structure
- strong reversal candle
- abnormal volatility
- news risk becomes extreme
- max daily risk nearly hit

6. /server/trade-management/monitoring/tradeHealthScore.ts

Return health score 0-100.

Factors:
- distance from stop
- distance from take profit
- structure support
- volatility state
- session quality
- spread state
- time in trade
- opposite signal risk

7. Frontend Live Trades page upgrade

For each open trade show:
- ticket
- symbol
- direction
- lot
- entry
- current price
- stop loss
- take profit
- floating P/L
- R multiple

- AI management suggestion

  - move to break even
