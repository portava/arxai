T023 — MANUAL MT5 V75 WORKS, FIX ARX/EA SYMBOL EXECUTION MISMATCH

Important correction:
The user can trade V75 directly from the MT5 terminal.

Therefore, the issue is not that V75 is unavailable, not tradable, or missing from Market Watch.

If manual MT5 trading works, then the broker account can trade the symbol. The failure is in the ARX → bridge → EA symbol resolution, pre-trade guard, or OrderSend preparation path.

Do not keep reporting “symbol not selectable” or “terminal/broker truth” as the final answer unless you prove why the EA cannot select the same exact symbol that MT5 manual trading can use.

Current confirmed facts:

1. ARX can place live EURUSD.
2. MT5 can manually trade V75.
3. ARX V75 command reaches the EA.
4. EA rejects before OrderSend with NULL retcode / EA_REJECTED_NO_DETAIL.
5. Therefore, the bug is likely in ARX symbol mapping, EA symbol resolution, EA pre-trade guard, or synthetic-specific OrderSend preparation.

Goal:
Make ARX place the same V75 symbol that the user can manually trade in MT5.

Do not use Demo.
Do not use Paper.
Do not fake execution.
