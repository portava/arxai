Actively attempt to break, stress, invalidate, and expose weaknesses in strategies, agent logic, execution assumptions, and behavioral models before any live promotion.

Adversarial tests must include:

1. Edge Fragility
- small parameter shifts
- delayed entries
- delayed exits
- reduced liquidity
- spread widening
- slippage increase

2. Regime Collapse
- strategy used in wrong regime
- sudden trend reversal
- volatility explosion
- chop transition
- liquidity collapse

3. Execution Sabotage
- partial fills
- latency spikes
- rejected orders
- broker instability
- execution delays

4. Behavioral Stress
- post-loss aggression
- override frequency
- fatigue conditions
- revenge-trading conditions
- overtrading patterns

5. Contradiction Testing
- conflicting agent votes
- stale signals
- incomplete data
- corrupted data
- misleading market structure

6. Overfit Exposure
- randomization
- shuffled trade order
- out-of-sample testing
- synthetic market variations
- hidden-regime evaluation

Rules:
- Validation must actively search for failure conditions.
- Strategies must prove robustness, not just profitability.
- Fragile strategies must be restricted, demoted, or retired.
- High profit with weak robustness cannot pass full promotion.
- All adversarial test results must be logged into the Black Box Vault.
- Replay Lab must support adversarial replay scenarios.
- Validation reports must explain exactly what broke the strategy and why.
