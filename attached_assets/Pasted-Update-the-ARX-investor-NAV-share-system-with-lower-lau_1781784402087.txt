Update the ARX investor NAV/share system with lower launch tiers and automatic growth pricing.

Goal:
Lower the share-tier thresholds so early growth is rewarded sooner, then make share prices adjust automatically as the pool scales.

Current profit split remains:
- Investors: 30% of total net realized profit
- Traders: 24.5% of total net realized profit
- ARX/company/platform: 45.5% of total net realized profit

Formula:
investor_pool = total_net_profit × 0.30
trader_pool = total_net_profit × 0.245
arx_company_share = total_net_profit × 0.455

Investor ownership:
investor_shares = deposit_amount ÷ active_share_price
investor_ownership_percent = investor_shares ÷ total_outstanding_shares
investor_profit_payout = investor_profit_pool × investor_ownership_percent

Automatic dynamic pricing after Tier 10:
- Once finalized NAV exceeds $1,500,000, calculate share price dynamically.
- Use $7.50 as the base Tier 10 price.
- For every additional $500,000 of finalized NAV above $1,500,000, increase the share price by a growth multiplier.
- Default growth multiplier: 20%.
- Admin may configure growth multiplier between 10% and 30%.
- Use finalized NAV only for official share issuance.
- Estimated NAV may show "estimated next price" but cannot activate it.

Dynamic formula:
extra_nav = max(0, finalized_nav - 1500000)
growth_steps = floor(extra_nav / 500000)
dynamic_share_price = 7.50 × (1 + growth_multiplier) ^ growth_steps

Default:
growth_multiplier = 0.20

Examples:
- $1,500,000 NAV: $7.50/share
- $2,000,000 NAV: $9.00/share
- $2,500,000 NAV: $10.80/share
- $3,000,000 NAV: $12.96/share
- $3,500,000 NAV: $15.55/share
- $4,000,000 NAV: $18.66/share

Rules:
1. Active share tier is based on finalized NAV, not floating NAV.
2. New deposits use the active finalized share price at approval/settlement.
3. Existing investors keep their shares.
4. Existing shares are never reduced when price increases.
5. Estimated NAV may show upcoming tier/price but must be labeled estimated.
6. Tier changes must write a ledger/audit record.
7. Share price should not decrease automatically after a loss unless admin enables downgrade mode.
8. Default behavior is stair-step upward pricing.
9. Withdrawals redeem shares at finalized NAV/share value, not estimated floating value.
10. Investors only see their own position; admin sees all.
