NEXT PHASE: FULL TESTER EXPERIENCE POLISH + SIMULATED LIVE MARKET ENGINE

Goal:
Now that the app is open for full tester access, improve the testing experience so every workflow feels complete even while MT5 is deferred.

The user should be able to test the app like a real trading platform:
- live chart visible
- trade ticket connected to selected chart symbol
- demo simulator feels live
- AI generates explainable trade ideas
- journal/calendar/audit logs update
- live tester workflows are open
- MT5-required broker execution remains deferred until MT5 bridge is connected

Do not fake real MT5 execution.
Do not claim real broker orders were placed.
Do not hide live pages.
Do not block tester workflows.

==================================================

Create an internal simulated market engine for tester/demo mode.

- synthetic price updates
- bid/ask spread
- candles
- current price
- session volatility
- mock fills
- simulated P/L
- stop loss hits
- take profit hits
- trailing position updates

Default symbols:

- EURUSD
- GBPUSD
- USDJPY
- XAUUSD
- BTCUSDT
- ETHUSDT
- AAPL
- TSLA

Create endpoints:

GET /api/market/symbols
GET /api/market/quote/:symbol
GET /api/market/candles/:symbol
GET /api/market/session-status
POST /api/market/simulator/start
POST /api/market/simulator/stop
POST /api/market/simulator/reset

The simulator should clearly show:

executionEnvironment = SIMULATOR
