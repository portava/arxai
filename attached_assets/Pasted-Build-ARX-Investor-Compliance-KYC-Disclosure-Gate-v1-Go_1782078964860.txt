Build ARX Investor Compliance + KYC + Disclosure Gate v1.

Goal:
Create a controlled investor onboarding and approval system before any user can fund an investor account, receive allocation, view funding instructions, or participate in the ARX investor pool.

This system should protect ARX by making sure every investor has:
- an InvestorAccount
- identity/KYC status
- risk disclosure acknowledgment
- investor profile
- admin approval
- deposit eligibility status
- audit trail

Important:
Do not allow public deposits.
Do not allow unapproved users to fund.
Do not allow users to bypass disclosures.
Do not allow investors to trade.
Do not use guaranteed-profit language.
Do not expose investor records to other investors.

Core rule:
A user cannot access crypto funding, manual funding instructions, investor allocations, or withdrawal features until their InvestorAccount is approved.

Investor onboarding flow:

1. User requests investor access
User submits:
- legal name
- email
- phone
- country/state
- date of birth if needed
- investor type:
  - individual
  - business/entity
- planned investment range
- funding preference:
  - crypto
  - bank/manual
  - undecided
- experience level:
  - beginner
  - intermediate
  - advanced
- risk acknowledgment checkbox

Status becomes:
- investor_access_requested

2. Investor disclosure screen
Before funding, user must acknowledge:
- trading involves risk of loss
- values are not guaranteed
- investor portal is internal tracking
- deposits do not become active capital until confirmed and approved
- withdrawals require admin approval
- lockup rules may apply
- crypto transfers may be irreversible
- ARX performance share applies only to profits if enabled

User must check:
- I understand trading involves risk of loss.
- I understand returns are not guaranteed.
- I understand withdrawals require review and approval.
- I understand crypto deposits are not credited until confirmed.
- I understand the investor portal is for internal tracking.

Save:
- disclosureVersion
- acceptedAt
- ip/device if available
- userId

5. Funding gate
Before creating any CryptoFundingIntent or manual deposit request, check:

Investor must have:
- InvestorAccount exists
- status = approved
- disclosure accepted
- KYC status = passed or manually approved
- deposit eligibility = eligible
- not suspended
- no compliance block

If any check fails:
- block funding
- show exact reason
- do not create funding intent

Example messages:
- “Investor approval is required before funding.”
- “Please accept the risk disclosure before funding.”
- “Your investor review is still pending.”
- “Funding is currently disabled for this account.”

6. Withdrawal gate
Investor can request withdrawal only if:
- InvestorAccount approved or previously approved
- allocation exists
- withdrawal not blocked by admin
- destination wallet/payment info exists
- amount is within eligible withdrawal amount

Suspended users:
- cannot add new deposits
- can still view existing records
- withdrawal requests require admin review

13. Security and privacy

Rules:
- investor can only access their own investor profile
- investor cannot view other investor records
- investor cannot change approval status
- investor cannot mark KYC passed
- investor cannot remove compliance hold
- investor cannot bypass disclosure
- admin-only routes require admin role
- sensitive investor info should not be exposed in public UI
- do not log full personal documents
- do not send private investor details to Ruby unless needed for a user-facing explanation

14. Required copy

Use:
- “Investor access request”
- “Risk disclosure”
- “Pending review”
- “Funding eligibility”
- “Approved for funding”
- “Risk of loss”
- “Not guaranteed”

Do not use:
- “Guaranteed returns”
- “Risk-free”
- “Instant approval”
- “Guaranteed profit”
- “Safe investment”

Disclosure text:
“Trading involves risk of loss. Values are not guaranteed. Deposits are not counted as active investor capital until confirmed and approved. Withdrawals require review and approval. This portal is for internal investor tracking.”
