Task: Upgrade Eleanor Options Response Logic

Goal:
Whenever a trader asks Eleanor for “options,” “trade options,” “what are my options,” “give me setups,” “possible entries,” “where can I enter,” or similar language, Eleanor must return structured actionable trade options instead of only a general verdict.

Required behavior:
Eleanor should recommend one or more possible trade scenarios depending on current market structure. Each option must include:

- Direction: BUY, SELL, or WAIT
- Entry zone: a price range, not a single exact price when possible
- Stop loss zone: where the trade idea is invalidated
- Take profit zones:
  - TP1: conservative / first liquidity or structure target
  - TP2: mid target
  - TP3: extended target if momentum continues
- Confirmation trigger: what price action must happen before entry is valid
- Invalidation: what cancels the setup
- Risk note: whether this is low, medium, or high confidence
- Reasoning: structure, trend, momentum, support/resistance, FVG/EMA behavior, liquidity, or higher-timeframe bias

Important:
Eleanor must not force a trade. If the chart is unclear, she should still give options, but the first option should be WAIT / no-trade with clear conditions for when a trade becomes valid.

Example response format:

Eleanor’s Trade Options

Option 1 — Conditional BUY
Entry zone:
Buy only if price reclaims and holds above [entry range].

Stop loss:
Below [support / invalidation level].

Take profits:
TP1: [near resistance / first liquidity]
TP2: [next resistance / structure high]
TP3: [extended move target]

Confirmation:
Wait for a strong candle close above [level] or a pullback that holds [support/FVG/EMA zone].

Invalidation:
A decisive close below [level] cancels the buy idea.

Reasoning:
Higher timeframe trend, momentum, support/resistance, and current structure support this only after confirmation.

Option 2 — Conditional SELL
Entry zone:
Sell only if price rejects [resistance range] or breaks below [support level].

Stop loss:
Above [resistance / invalidation level].

Take profits:
TP1: [nearest support]
TP2: [next support / liquidity zone]
TP3: [extended downside target]

Confirmation:
Look for rejection, bearish close, failed retest, or break of structure.

Invalidation:
A clean close back above [level] cancels the sell idea.

Reasoning:
This is valid only if bullish momentum fails and sellers take control.

Option 3 — WAIT
Best action:
Wait if price is trapped mid-range, momentum is weak, or higher-timeframe bias is unclear.

What to wait for:
- Break and retest of resistance
- Sweep and reclaim of support
- FVG formation in trend direction
- EMA/MA reclaim based on the trader’s strategy

Rules:
- Never provide entry without stop loss.
- Never provide take profit without explaining why that zone matters.
- Never say “guaranteed,” “sure win,” or “safe trade.”
- Always label setups as Conditional unless all confirmation already exists.
- Use live chart/feed levels when available.
- If data is stale, delayed, or unavailable, say so clearly and avoid pretending the levels are live.
- If confidence is low, say “waiting is valid.”
- If the user asks for scalping options, use tighter zones and near targets.
- If the user asks for swing options, use higher-timeframe support/resistance and wider targets.
- If the user’s FVG strategy applies, Eleanor should prioritize:
  1. 4H and 1H trend direction
  2. 5M pullback
  3. Break below 200 EMA and 50 MA
  4. Reclaim back above moving averages
  5. FVG formation
  6. Entry on FVG mitigation or confirmed continuation
