Rules:
- Approved strategies do not automatically receive equal allocation.
- Allocation must consider validation quality, edge durability, execution quality, decision quality, survival score, market regime fit, trader behavior risk, confidence health, and strategy trust score.
- Portfolio allocation must adapt to ecosystem health.
- High disagreement, poor execution quality, weak confidence health, elevated cognitive risk, or market danger can reduce global aggression.
- Reserve allocation must dynamically expand during dangerous conditions.
- Allocation must prioritize survivability-adjusted efficiency, not raw return.
- Strategies compete dynamically for allocation.
- Capital fatigue and overdeployment must reduce participation.
- Exposure Balancer must reduce correlated risk.
- Portfolio Health Engine must continuously monitor concentration, fragility, diversification, execution vulnerability, behavioral vulnerability, and regime concentration.
- Liquidity-aware deployment must reduce sizing during poor liquidity conditions.
- Portfolio Manager cannot bypass Risk Governor or Control Tower.
- All allocation changes, capital climate changes, reserve shifts, and portfolio health changes must be logged into the Black Box Vault.
- Keep all previous phase tests passing.
- No unrestricted live auto-execution yet.
