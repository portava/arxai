Validation must grade:

- market regime fit
- execution reality
- trader behavior impact
- edge durability
- adversarial robustness
- confidence health
- system health

Rules:
- A strategy cannot pass validation based on profit alone.
- Strong profit with dangerous drawdown must fail or be restricted.
- Good backtest with poor out-of-sample performance must fail.
- Good signal quality with poor execution quality must be restricted.
- Regime-specific strategies must be labeled and limited to approved regimes.
- Edge decay triggers demotion, quarantine, or review.
- Monte Carlo tests must randomize trade order, slippage, spread, and execution delay.
- Adversarial validation must actively try to break strategies.
- Continuous validation must keep monitoring approved strategies after promotion.
- Evidence decay must reduce reliance on old results.
- Live sanity checks can cancel execution before entry.
- Risk Governor can freeze promotion.
- Control Tower controls rollout authority.
- All validation reports, trust changes, quarantines, failures, and promotions must be logged into the Black Box Vault.
- Keep all previous phase tests passing.
- No unrestricted live auto-execution yet.
