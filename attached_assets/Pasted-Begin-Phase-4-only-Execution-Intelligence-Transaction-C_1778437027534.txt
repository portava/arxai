Rules:
- Execution Intelligence cannot place trades directly.
- It can recommend EXECUTE, WAIT, REDUCE_SIZE, LIMIT_ONLY, CANCEL, SOFT_BLOCK, or HARD_BLOCK.
- Every proposed trade must receive a preTradeCostEstimate.
- Every filled trade must receive a postTradeExecutionReport.
- Compare:
  - expected fill vs actual fill
  - decision price vs execution price
  - spread at signal vs spread at fill
  - latency at decision vs latency at fill
  - broker health before vs after execution
- If estimated execution cost destroys the trade’s expected edge, block the trade.
- If execution quality is poor, reduce size or require limit orders.
- If broker reliability is degraded, force WAIT, REDUCE_SIZE, or LOCKDOWN depending on severity.
- ExecutionRiskScore must be consumed by Risk Governor.
- ExecutionHealth must be consumed by Control Tower.
- All execution intelligence records must be logged into the Black Box Vault.
- Keep all previous phase tests passing.
- No unrestricted live auto-execution yet.

Required outputs:
- preTradeCostEstimate
- postTradeExecutionReport
- executionRiskScore
- executionQualityGrade
- implementationShortfall
- brokerReliabilityScore
- recommendedExecutionAction
- plainEnglishExplanation

Execution verdicts:
- EXECUTION_CLEAN
- EXECUTION_ACCEPTABLE
- EXECUTION_COSTLY
- EXECUTION_UNSTABLE
- EXECUTION_BLOCKED
