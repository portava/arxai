2. Account is LIVE-enabled.
3. LIVE broker execution remains enabled.
4. MT5 bridge is connected.
5. EA/bridge heartbeat healthy.
6. Kill switch inactive.
7. Account not frozen.
8. Allocation available.
9. Symbol allowed.

12. Lot size equals broker minimum or configured micro-test size.

2. Use the smallest allowed lot size.
3. Use the configured live-test direction if present.
4. If no live-test direction is configured, pause and require owner/admin selection in the UI.
5. Press the real Confirm button once.
6. Confirm pending state appears immediately.
7. Confirm backend request fires.
8. Confirm MT5 ticket/order is returned.
9. Confirm ARX ledger records the trade.
10. Confirm Scanner/chart shows the trade only for the authorized owner/admin.
11. Confirm Ruby can explain the opened position in plain English without exposing internals.
12. Close the trade immediately.
13. Confirm MT5 close result.
14. Confirm ARX ledger marks the trade closed.
15. Confirm Scanner/chart removes or marks the trade closed.
16. Record ticket/order ID, symbol, lot, open time, close time, and P/L.
17. Confirm arx_live_commands changed only by expected open/close commands.
18. Do not run a second trade.
