New task: Deep audit Auto Trading Bot intelligence, readiness, and performance safety.

Goal:
Verify whether the ARX auto trading bot is actually intelligent enough to perform safely and whether it can make high-quality trading decisions without blindly following weak scanner/scalp signals.

This is an audit-first task. Do not enable, expand, or loosen auto trading until the findings prove it is safe.

Systems to inspect:
1. Auto trading bot / AI auto mode
2. Ruby trade decision engine
3. Scanner signal intake
4. Scalp Builder / Flare system intake
5. Trade Health / Eligibility contract
6. Live execution gate stack
7. Risk governance
8. MT5 bridge dispatch
9. Position monitoring
10. Post-trade review / learning loop
11. Emergency stop / kill switch
12. Account exposure controls

Core question:
Can the bot independently decide:
- when NOT to trade
- when scanner signals are weak
- when scalp signals conflict with chart read
- when feed data is stale or unconfirmed
- when risk is already stacked
- when news/calendar data is unavailable
- when open positions make a new trade dangerous
- when a trade should be skipped even if a setup looks attractive

Audit requirements:
1. Decision intelligence:
   - Verify what inputs the bot uses before proposing or placing a trade.
   - Confirm it reads chart structure, trend, momentum, support/resistance, HTF context, spread, slippage, news risk, existing exposure, and trade health.
   - Confirm it does not trade from one signal alone.
   - Confirm it can say "NO TRADE" even when Scanner/Scalp Builder says Ready now.

2. Feed truth:
   - Auto bot must not trade when data is:
     - historical-only
     - feed-limited
     - stale
     - not AI-usable
     - not broker-live
     - synthetic/composite without broker confirmation
   - Confirm bot uses the same feed verdict as Scanner/Ruby/chart.
   - Confirm feed confidence can only downgrade, never grant execution permission.

3. Scanner/Scalp reconciliation:
   - If Chart Read says low confidence/wait, but Scalp Builder says Valid now, bot must downgrade to conditional or skip.
   - If price is near support/resistance and scalp has limited room, bot must reduce confidence.
   - If scalp signal depends on unconfirmed continuation, bot must wait.
   - Bot must not treat “Ready now” wording as enough to execute.

4. Risk intelligence:
   - Bot must account for:
     - daily loss
     - weekly loss
     - max open trades
     - max exposure per symbol
     - stacked same-direction positions
     - no-stop-loss positions
     - margin/free margin
     - floating drawdown
     - correlated exposure
   - If the user already has stacked risk, bot should reduce or block new exposure.
   - If open positions are weakening, bot should not add blindly.

5. Execution gating:
   - Confirm every auto trade still passes all live execution gates.
   - Bot intelligence must never bypass:
     - kill switch
     - per-user arming
     - bridge health
     - broker connection
     - allowed symbols
     - lot limits
     - margin checks
     - stop-loss policy where applicable
     - owner/admin live path rules
   - Display confidence must never directly execute.

6. Trade planning:
   For every proposed auto trade, bot must produce:
   - direction
   - entry logic
   - stop-loss
   - take-profit
   - risk amount
   - expected reward
   - reward:risk
   - invalidation
   - reason for trade
   - reason not to trade
   - data/feed status
   - confidence
   - expiration time

7. Post-trade intelligence:
   After every trade closes, bot must explain:
   - why it entered
   - what happened
   - whether the thesis was right or wrong
   - whether the loss/win was due to setup quality, timing, spread, news, volatility, or risk
   - what it learned
   - whether this strategy should be promoted, demoted, or paused

8. Performance testing:
   Run deterministic backtests/simulations against:
   - trending market
   - ranging market
   - news spike
   - low-liquidity session
   - high spread
   - stale feed
   - synthetic market feed-limited state
   - conflicting scanner/scalp/chart reads
   - stacked open exposure
   - drawdown day
   - bridge disconnected

9. Pass/fail scoring:
   Grade the bot in these categories:
   - Market read intelligence: /100
   - Feed-truth honesty: /100
   - Risk control: /100
   - Execution safety: /100
   - Explanation quality: /100
   - Performance consistency: /100
   - Stop/skip discipline: /100
   - Overall readiness: /100

10. Readiness levels:
   Classify the bot as one of:
   - Not ready for auto trading
   - Ready for demo only
   - Ready for supervised micro-live only
   - Ready for limited live automation
   - Ready for full auto under governance

Important:
Do not just check if the bot can place trades.
Check whether it SHOULD place trades.

Required evidence:
- list of files/modules inspected
- full decision flow diagram
- every input the bot uses
- every gate before execution
- every place the bot can be stopped
- every place weak data is downgraded
- examples of trades it would take
- examples of trades it correctly skips
- failure modes
- missing tests
- recommended fixes

Tests to add or verify:
1. Bot skips when feed is historical-only.
2. Bot skips when Ruby Chart Read confidence is low and Scalp Builder is only conditional.
3. Bot skips when economic/news provider is missing and event risk is unknown.
4. Bot skips or reduces risk when existing exposure is stacked.
5. Bot skips when bridge heartbeat is stale.
6. Bot skips when margin/free margin is unsafe.
7. Bot cannot execute from display-only “Ready now” wording.
8. Bot cannot bypass live execution gates.
9. Bot generates a complete trade plan before execution.
10. Bot writes post-trade review after close.
11. Bot handles losing streak by reducing aggression or stopping.
12. Bot handles spread/slippage widening.
13. Bot handles no stop-loss policy correctly.
14. Bot handles owner/admin live path without bypassing broker truth.
15. Bot produces deterministic audit logs for every decision.

Hard boundary:
Do not enable full auto trading.
Do not loosen risk gates.
Do not bypass broker dispatch checks.
Do not place live trades during audit unless explicitly approved as a supervised micro-lot test.
Do not change owner/admin permissions unless a bug is found and approved.
This task is audit, scoring, test coverage, and safety-readiness only.
