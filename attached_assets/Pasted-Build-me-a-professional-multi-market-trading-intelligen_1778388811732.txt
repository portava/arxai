Strategies:
Create modular strategies:

1. Trend Continuation
Uses EMA 20, EMA 50, EMA 200, RSI, ATR.

2. Break of Structure
Uses swing highs/lows and close confirmation.

3. Pullback Continuation
Waits for price to return to EMA zone or support/resistance.

4. Liquidity Sweep Reversal
Detects stop hunt above/below recent highs/lows.

5. Volatility Expansion
Uses ATR expansion and strong candle close.

6. Mean Reversion
Only active in confirmed range conditions.

7. Session Breakout
For London open and New York open.

8. News Avoidance Mode
Blocks forex/indices trades near major economic news.
