Before creating the final roadmap, upgrade the weak areas of the AI Trading Operating System.

Strengthen the ecosystem in the areas that currently limit real-world performance: execution realism, attention/UX, complexity control, explainability, infrastructure resilience, cognitive awareness, and stress testing.

Global rules:
- These layers should not place live trades directly.
- Execution Microstructure can block or reduce trade size.
- Attention Engine controls what the UI emphasizes.
- Complexity Governor can disable nonessential agents during high latency or overload.
- Explainability Layer must generate plain-English reasoning.
- Resilience Engine can force DEGRADED_MODE, LOCKDOWN, or SAFE_SHUTDOWN.
- Cognitive Engine can force cooldown, recovery mode, or reduced permissions.
- Stress Lab is simulation-only.
- All outputs must be logged into the Black Box Vault.
- Risk Governor and Control Tower remain above all systems.
