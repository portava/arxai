Update the ARX AI Mission Planner so users can set the target timeframe in minutes, hours, or days.

Goal:
Users should be able to tell the trading bot how fast they want to reach a target amount, such as:
- 5 minutes
- 10 minutes
- 20 minutes
- 30 minutes
- 1 hour
- 2 hours
- 5 hours
- 10 hours
- 1 day
- 3 days
- 1 week
- Custom timeframe

Current issue:
The planner currently uses "Timeframe (days)" only. That is too limited. The user needs to be able to define short-term trading missions and scalping missions using minutes or hours.

Required UI changes:

1. Replace the existing "Timeframe (days)" input with a two-part timeframe selector:

Field A:
- Timeframe amount
- Numeric input
- Examples: 5, 20, 30, 1, 5, 10

Field B:
- Timeframe unit
- Dropdown:
  - Minutes
  - Hours
  - Days
  - Weeks

Example display:
Timeframe: [30] [Minutes]
Timeframe: [5] [Hours]
Timeframe: [1] [Day]

2. Add quick-pick chips above or below the input:
- 5 min
- 10 min
- 20 min
- 30 min
- 1 hr
- 2 hr
- 5 hr
- 10 hr
- 1 day
- 3 days
- 1 week

When a user taps a chip, it should populate the amount and unit fields.

3. Store timeframe internally in minutes.

Add/normalize these values:
- timeframeAmount
- timeframeUnit
- timeframeMinutes
- timeframeLabel

Examples:
- 5 minutes = timeframeAmount 5, timeframeUnit "minutes", timeframeMinutes 5, timeframeLabel "5 minutes"
- 20 minutes = timeframeMinutes 20
- 30 minutes = timeframeMinutes 30
- 1 hour = timeframeMinutes 60
- 5 hours = timeframeMinutes 300
- 10 hours = timeframeMinutes 600
- 1 day = timeframeMinutes 1440
- 1 week = timeframeMinutes 10080

4. Update feasibility math.

The planner should calculate required return based on the exact timeframe.

Example:
Starting amount: $50
Target amount: $100
Required total return: 100%

If timeframe is 1 day:
Required pace = 100% per day

If timeframe is 10 hours:
Required pace should be normalized and displayed as:
- 100% total required return
- 10% per hour average pace
- 240% per day equivalent pace

If timeframe is 30 minutes:
Required pace should show:
- 100% total required return
- 200% per hour equivalent pace
- 4,800% per day equivalent pace

5. Add timeframe-specific mission labels.

If timeframe unit is minutes:
- classify as "Scalp mission"
- if target is very aggressive, classify as "Extreme scalp mission"
- if impossible/unreasonable, classify as "Unrealistic scalp mission"

If timeframe unit is hours:
- classify as "Intraday mission"
- if aggressive, classify as "High-risk intraday mission"
- if unreasonable, classify as "Unrealistic intraday mission"

If timeframe unit is days/weeks:
- classify as "Swing mission" or "Multi-day mission"

6. Add better warnings for short timeframes.

For minute-based missions, show:
"Minute-based missions are extremely sensitive to spread, slippage, candle confirmation, execution speed, and minimum lot sizing."

For 5–30 minute targets, show:
"Very short missions require confirmed live feed, fast execution, and realistic target sizing. The bot may reject targets that require unsafe or unrealistic returns."

For small accounts under $100, keep this warning:
"Small balances have less room for drawdown and may be more affected by spread, fees, and minimum lot sizing."

7. Live safety rules must remain strict.

Do not allow the new timeframe options to bypass any live trading gate.

The bot must still require:
- confirmed broker feed
- valid symbol feed
- risk engine approval
- user/bot permissions
- bridge status healthy
- market open / tradable condition
- allowed symbol
- lot sizing valid
- max drawdown rules
- kill switch off
- no stale candle data
- no simulator-only feed for live missions

8. Add bot instruction support.

The mission parser should understand natural language like:
- "Turn $50 into $100 in 30 minutes"
- "Make $200 in 5 hours"
- "Grow this to $500 by tomorrow"
- "Make this amount in 10 hours"
- "Scalp this account to $100 in 20 minutes"

The parser should extract:
- startingAmount
- targetAmount
- timeframeAmount
- timeframeUnit
- timeframeMinutes
- riskProfile if mentioned

9. Update saved mission records.

Mission records should include:
- startingAmount
- targetAmount
- timeframeAmount
- timeframeUnit
- timeframeMinutes
- timeframeLabel
- riskProfile
- feasibilityScore
- riskScore
- missionType
- feedStatus
- status: draft / active / rejected / completed / failed

10. Rejection rules.

Reject or draft-only missions when:
- timeframeMinutes is too short for the selected symbol/timeframe
- required return is outside supported limits
- feed is not confirmed
- sample size is 0 and bot would need live execution
- risk engine marks the mission unsafe
- mission requires unrealistic daily/hourly return

Do not delete the mission. Save it as an unrealistic draft with explanation.

11. UI copy updates.

Replace:
"Timeframe (days)"

With:
"Timeframe"

Add helper text:
"Choose how fast you want the bot to attempt the target. Shorter timeframes increase risk and may be rejected by the safety engine."

On result cards, show:
- Required total return
- Required pace per hour if under 24 hours
- Required daily equivalent pace
- Mission type
- Feed status
- Start eligibility

12. Tests.

Add tests for:
- 5 minutes converts to 5 timeframeMinutes
- 20 minutes converts to 20 timeframeMinutes
- 30 minutes converts to 30 timeframeMinutes
- 1 hour converts to 60 timeframeMinutes
- 5 hours converts to 300 timeframeMinutes
- 10 hours converts to 600 timeframeMinutes
- 1 day converts to 1440 timeframeMinutes
- quick-pick chips populate the correct fields
- natural language parser extracts "30 minutes"
- natural language parser extracts "5 hours"
- $50 to $100 in 30 minutes is classified unrealistic/extreme
- feed not confirmed blocks live start
- unrealistic missions save as draft only
- no live execution gate is bypassed

Acceptance criteria:
- Users can choose minutes, hours, days, or weeks.
- Quick chips work.
- Mission planner stores timeframe in minutes internally.
- Risk and feasibility calculations update based on exact timeframe.
- Short scalping missions are clearly labeled and warned.
- Unrealistic missions can be saved as drafts but cannot start.
- Live trading safety gates remain fully enforced.
