3. Trend detection rules

Bullish HTF trend:
4H and 1H should both confirm upward bias using a balanced method:
- Price above 200 EMA
- 50 MA or 50 EMA above 200 EMA, or trending upward
- Recent structure has higher highs / higher lows where possible
- Recent candle closes support upward continuation
- Avoid calling it bullish if price is chopping directly through the 200 EMA with no structure

Bearish HTF trend:
- Price below 200 EMA
- 50 MA or 50 EMA below 200 EMA, or trending downward
- Recent structure has lower highs / lower lows where possible
- Recent candle closes support downward continuation
- Avoid calling it bearish if price is chopping directly through the 200 EMA with no structure

Trend verdicts:
- strong_uptrend
- weak_uptrend
- neutral
- weak_downtrend
- strong_downtrend

The strategy should require:

- bearish setup: 4H = strong_downtrend or weak_downtrend AND 1H = strong_downtrend or weak_downtrend

4. 5M pullback rules

Bullish setup:
After HTF bullish alignment:
- Detect that price pulled back below the 50 MA and 200 EMA on 5M.
- Then detect that price broke/reclaimed back above both the 50 MA and 200 EMA.
- Reclaim should be candle-close based, not only wick based.
- Stronger score if reclaim candle has impulse body, closes near high, or breaks recent minor structure.

Bearish setup:
After HTF bearish alignment:
- Detect that price pulled back above the 50 MA and 200 EMA on 5M.
- Then detect that price broke/rejected back below both the 50 MA and 200 EMA.
- Rejection should be candle-close based, not only wick based.
- Stronger score if rejection candle has impulse body, closes near low, or breaks recent minor structure.

- invalidated

5. FVG detection rules

Bullish FVG:
Use standard 3-candle imbalance logic:
- Candle 1 high is below Candle 3 low
- Gap between Candle 1 high and Candle 3 low creates bullish FVG zone
- Candle 2 should ideally be displacement/impulse candle
- FVG should form after MA reclaim, not before
- Prefer fresh/unmitigated FVGs
- Track whether price has returned into the FVG

Bullish FVG zone:
- fvgLow = candle1.high
- fvgHigh = candle3.low
- midpoint = 50% of FVG zone

Bearish FVG:
- Candle 1 low is above Candle 3 high
- Gap between Candle 1 low and Candle 3 high creates bearish FVG zone
- Candle 2 should ideally be displacement/impulse candle
- FVG should form after MA rejection/loss, not before
- Prefer fresh/unmitigated FVGs

Bearish FVG zone:
- fvgLow = candle3.high
- fvgHigh = candle1.low
- midpoint = 50% of FVG zone

FVG states:
- fresh
- partially_filled
- fully_mitigated
- invalidated

Do not use an already fully mitigated FVG as a fresh entry signal.
