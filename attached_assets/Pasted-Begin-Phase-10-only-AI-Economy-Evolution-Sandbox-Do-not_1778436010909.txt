Rules:
- Agents earn or lose reputation based on evidence, not opinions.
- Strategies earn or lose trust based on validation, replay, live micro-results, survival score, execution quality, and decision quality.
- Weak strategies move to quarantine, demotion, retirement, or archive.
- Strong strategies can gain more testing priority or controlled allocation.
- Evolution and mutation are sandbox-only.
- No evolved strategy can skip validation.
- No mutation can touch live money directly.
- Survival quality matters more than raw profit.
- Resource management prioritizes the most valuable tests, replays, and validations.
- Risk Governor and Control Tower outrank all AI Economy decisions.
- All reputation changes, lifecycle transitions, mutations, and sandbox results must be logged into the Black Box Vault.

Strategy lifecycle stages:
- RESEARCH
- TESTING
- SHADOW
- PAPER
- MICRO
- LIMITED_LIVE
- ACTIVE
- UNDER_REVIEW
- QUARANTINED
- DEGRADED
- RETIRED
- ARCHIVED

Required outputs:
- agentReputationScore
- strategyTrustScore
- survivalCurrencyScore
- lifecycleStage
- promotionEligibility
- demotionRisk
- mutationCandidate
- sandboxValidationResult
- resourcePriority

Acceptance criteria:
- Agents and strategies gain/lose trust from measurable performance.
- Strategy lifecycle transitions work.
- Weak systems can be quarantined or retired.
- Evolution runs only in sandbox.
- Mutated strategies must enter validation from the beginning.
- All previous tests still pass.
- No unrestricted live auto-execution is enabled.
