T025 — REMOVE LIVE STATUS BILLBOARD BANNERS / REPLACE WITH SIMPLE LIVE CHIP

Current issue:
The Scanner page has large live-status banners taking up too much space. The user circled the top live/shared master warning banner and said:

“Remove all banners. Not a billboard. A simple live will do.”

Screenshot shows a large horizontal banner with:

* “Live (Shared Master MT5)”
* “Live trading is active on your shared-maste…”
* “REAL MONEY RISK”
* large “Live (Shared)” button/chip
* plus another “Live (Shared Master MT5)” chip below

This is too bulky and repetitive for a high-speed trading app.

Scope:
This task is only for removing/collapsing repetitive live-status banners and replacing them with a compact live indicator.

Do not change live execution.
Do not change Governance.
Do not change permissions.
Do not change MT5 bridge behavior.
Do not change Ruby.
Do not place trades.
Do not use Demo/Paper/fake data.

Primary goal:
Remove billboard-style live banners across the app and replace them with a small, clean LIVE status chip.

PART 1 — Audit all live/demo/risk banners

Search the app for all large banners/cards/alerts that show:

1. Live (Shared Master MT5)
2. Live trading is active
3. Real money risk
4. Live (Shared)
5. Shared Master MT5 status banners
6. Demo mode banners
7. Paper Trading banners
8. Read-only/live locked banners
9. repeated account mode banners
10. risk disclosure billboard cards on trading screens

Find all components/files rendering these banners, especially on:

1. Scanner
2. Cockpit
3. Trade page
4. Ruby panel
5. More/settings
6. Admin/live pages
7. Mobile layout header

PART 2 — Replace with compact status chip

Use one small status indicator instead of large banners.

Required compact chip:

1. text: “LIVE”
2. optional subtext only if needed: “Shared MT5”
3. small shield/lightning/dot icon if already available
4. color: existing live green style
5. tap/click opens account/live details if needed
6. no large red billboard
7. no repeated full-width banner

Example:
“● LIVE · Shared MT5”

or:
“LIVE”

The chip should appear in the top header/account status area only once per page.

PART 3 — Remove repetitive risk billboard

Remove the large “REAL MONEY RISK” banner from active trading screens.

Replace with:

1. compact “LIVE” chip
2. optional small tooltip/help text on tap:
    “Live trading uses real funds.”
3. optional More/Legal/Risk page for full disclosure if needed

Do not show giant risk text on the trading surface every time.
Do not make Scanner feel like a billboard.

PART 4 — Keep important warning behavior only when needed

Do not remove meaningful alerts when there is an actual problem.

Keep compact warnings for:

1. MT5 bridge disconnected
2. EA heartbeat stale
3. broker not tradable
4. account frozen
5. kill switch active
6. governance block
7. order rejected
8. insufficient funds
9. market closed
10. symbol unavailable

But these should be compact and contextual, not permanent billboard banners.

PART 5 — Remove duplicated live chips

The screenshot shows live status in multiple places.

1. Only one main live chip should appear in the page header.
2. Do not repeat “Live (Shared Master MT5)” directly under another live banner.
3. Do not show both “Live (Shared Master MT5)” and “Live (Shared)” unless one is hidden inside details.
4. Account status details can live in a collapsible drawer or account panel.
