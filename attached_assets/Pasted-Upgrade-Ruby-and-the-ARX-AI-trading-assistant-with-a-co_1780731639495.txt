Upgrade Ruby and the ARX AI trading assistant with a complete Trading Mode + Trade Accountability System.

This is an upgrade to the existing ARX AI app. Do NOT rebuild the app. Do NOT break existing routes, hooks, MT5 bridge logic, scanner logic, Ruby chat, execution services, admin permissions, user permissions, live shared trading, risk controls, or existing UI wiring. The goal:
Ruby must never take, recommend, queue, or assist with a trade without clearly explaining the trade plan first. Ruby must state:
1. Why the trade is being considered.
2. What trading mode is being used.
3. Entry logic.
4. Direction: Buy or Sell.
5. Expected loss if wrong.
6. Expected profit if right.
7. Risk/reward ratio.
8. Stop loss plan.
9. Take profit plan.
10. Invalidation point.
11. Confidence score.
12. What could make Ruby change her mind.
13. What market conditions must remain true for the trade to stay valid.

After the trade closes, Ruby must explain:
1. What happened.
2. Whether the trade followed the original plan.
3. Whether SL, TP, manual close, trailing stop, partial close, or reversal logic caused exit.
4. Why the trade won or lost.
5. What Ruby saw correctly.
6. What Ruby missed.
7. What should be adjusted next time.
8. Whether the mode was appropriate.
9. Whether the entry was early, late, or accurate.
10. Whether risk was respected.
11. Whether the trade should be used for AI learning.

Add Trading Modes:

A. Conservative Mode
Purpose:
Protect capital first. Only take high-quality setups.

Behavior:
- Requires stronger confirmation.
- Lower lot sizing.
- Wider rejection of weak signals.
- Avoids trades near major news unless news is favorable and confirmed.
- Requires cleaner trend, support/resistance, liquidity, and structure alignment.
- Lower maximum open positions.
- Prefers higher probability trades over fast scalps.
- Must clearly state: “This is a Conservative setup because…”

Required logic:
- Higher confidence threshold.
- Stronger multi-timeframe agreement.
- Avoid overtrading.
- Reject unclear markets.
- Prefer risk/reward of at least 1:1.5 unless scalp rules justify otherwise.
- Must explain why risk is acceptable.

B. Balanced Mode
Purpose:
Default mode. Good balance between opportunity and protection.

Behavior:
- Allows normal AI-assisted trading.
- Requires reasonable confirmation.
- Allows trend trades, pullbacks, breakouts, and clean scalps.
- Uses moderate lot sizing based on user caps and bridge rules.
- Must explain: “This is a Balanced setup because…”

Required logic:
- Medium confidence threshold.
- Uses market structure, trend, momentum, volatility, support/resistance, candle behavior, spread, slippage, and news.
- Risk/reward target should usually be at least 1:1.2 or better unless the trade is a valid short scalp.

C. Aggressive Mode
Purpose:
Faster entries and more opportunity, but still controlled and explainable.

Behavior:
- Allows earlier entries.
- Allows momentum continuation.
- Allows faster scalps.
- Allows more frequent signals.
- Uses tighter invalidation and quicker reassessment.
- Must NEVER become reckless or random.
- Must explain: “This is an Aggressive setup because…”

Required logic:
- Lower confirmation threshold than Conservative/Balanced.
- Strong focus on live momentum, candle flame, volatility expansion, order-flow proxy, breakout continuation, and fast rejection zones.
- Must clearly state that the trade has higher risk.
- Must calculate estimated drawdown before entry.
- Must state where Ruby is wrong.
- Must still respect user/admin risk settings and bridge limits unless admin override already exists in the app.

D. Scalping Mode
Purpose:
Fast short-term trades from one candle to several candles.

Behavior:
- Uses flame/momentum detection.
- Looks for fast candle expansion, clean directional pressure, repeated rejection, spread acceptability, volatility burst, and quick TP zones.
- Allows multi-trade scalp entries only when the flame continues and risk allows.
- Must explain whether this is a single-entry scalp or multi-entry scalp.
- Must calculate each entry’s planned loss/profit separately and combined basket risk.

Required logic:
- Entry speed matters.
- Detect candle “run-on flame” movement.
- TP can be smaller, but must be justified.
- SL must be very clear.
- Must reject scalp if spread/slippage is too high.
- Must say whether trade is expected to last one candle, several candles, or until momentum weakens.
- Must explain after exit whether momentum continued, faded, reversed, or trapped entry.

E. News-Aware Mode
Purpose:
Trades that are affected by news, calendar events, speeches, market shocks, or volatility events.

Behavior:
- Ruby must show upcoming news or active news directly in the trade explanation.
- Ruby must explain if news supports, conflicts with, or increases danger for the trade.
- If news is near, Ruby must say whether she is waiting, entering early, entering after confirmation, or avoiding.
- News alerts should connect to the chart and scanner if those systems already exist.

Required logic:
- Pull from existing news/calendar sources.
- If data source is unavailable, Ruby must say: “News source unavailable, reducing confidence.”
- Do not fabricate news.
- Must show the trade impact clearly.

F. Custom Mode
Purpose:
Allow admin/user-defined trading behavior.

Add settings:
- Mode name
- Max risk per trade
- Max open trades
- Minimum confidence
- Preferred RR
- Scalping allowed true/false
- News trading allowed true/false
- Multi-trade allowed true/false
- Max lot
- Max basket exposure
- Auto-close behavior
- Reversal behavior
- Conservative confirmation required true/false

Mode selection UI:
Add a clean Trading Mode selector wherever Ruby or scanner trading decisions happen:
- Ruby chat
- Scanner page
- Market view/chart
- Trade ticket if one exists
- Admin/user settings if applicable

Do not clutter the UI. Use a compact segmented selector or dropdown:
Conservative | Balanced | Aggressive | Scalping | News-Aware | Custom

The selected mode must be stored per user and used consistently across:
