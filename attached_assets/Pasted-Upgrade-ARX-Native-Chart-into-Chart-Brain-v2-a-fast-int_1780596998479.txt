Upgrade ARX Native Chart into Chart Brain v2: a fast, intelligent, self-critical, Ruby-aware, agent-aware decision surface.

This is not a rebuild.

Do not pass this task unless the chart is actually doing what it is supposed to do. If something fails, rerun, fix, retest, and only pass when the full flow is working.
