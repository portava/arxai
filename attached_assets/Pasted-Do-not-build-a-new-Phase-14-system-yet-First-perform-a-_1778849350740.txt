Step 3 — Adjustment rules

Only after the audit, make minimal adjustments.

Rules:
1. Do not rebuild the assistant from scratch.
2. Do not create duplicate tools if similar tools already exist.
3. Do not rename working routes/components unless absolutely necessary.
4. Do not change global UI design.
5. Do not weaken paper-only/liveLocked safety.
6. Do not expose MT5_BRIDGE_TOKEN, SESSION_SECRET, apiKeyHash, password hashes, API keys, bridge credentials, or hidden secrets.
7. Do not add fake market data, fake balances, fake trades, fake heartbeat, or fake user history.
8. Do not enable live trade execution.
9. Do not use ts-ignore, any, skipLibCheck, or temporary suppression to hide errors.
10. If a feature already exists, improve/wire the existing one instead of replacing it.
