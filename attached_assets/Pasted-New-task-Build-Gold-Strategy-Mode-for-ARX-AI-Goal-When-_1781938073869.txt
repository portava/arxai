SECTION 4 — Gold technical tactics

Gold Mode should prioritize these tactics:

1. Liquidity sweep + reclaim
Best for:
- reversals
- scalps
- trap detection

Bullish version:
- price sweeps below prior low/support/Asian low
- fails to continue lower
- reclaims level
- confirms with close/retest
- target toward midpoint, pivot, prior high, or liquidity

Bearish version:
- price sweeps above prior high/resistance/Asian high
- fails to continue higher
- rejects/reclaims lower
- confirms with close/retest
- target toward midpoint, pivot, prior low, or liquidity

2. Breakout + retest
Gold breakouts often fake out, so Gold Mode should prefer:
- close beyond level
- retest hold/rejection
- momentum confirmation
- spread acceptable
- not into immediate opposing level

3. Trend pullback
For trending gold:
- identify HTF direction
- wait for pullback to structure/pivot/trendline
- confirm rejection/continuation
- use ATR-based stop beyond invalidation
- avoid chasing extension

4. Wick rejection at key level
Gold wick behavior is important.
Valid only if:
- wick occurs at strong level
- body closes back inside/rejects
- next candle confirms
- target room exists

5. News impulse continuation/fade
Only after news window behavior is classified:
- first spike often unreliable
- wait for spread normalization
- wait for candle close/retest
- classify as continuation, reversal, or no-trade

6. Range edge bounce
Valid only at range edge:
- no midpoint entries
- require rejection candle
- target midpoint/other side
- avoid if range is compressing before news

7. Exhaustion fade
Gold can overextend fast.
Fade only if:
- ATR extension extreme
- reversal structure appears
- wick rejection appears
- support/resistance/pivot agrees
- confirmation appears
Otherwise mark “too late, do not chase.”

SECTION 5 — Gold candlestick emphasis

Gold Mode should weight these candles strongly when they appear at major levels:

Bearish:
- shooting star
- bearish engulfing
- bearish pin bar
- evening star
- tweezer top
- long upper wick rejection
- bearish outside bar

Bullish:
- hammer
- bullish engulfing
- bullish pin bar
- morning star
- tweezer bottom
- long lower wick rejection
- bullish outside bar

Shooting Star Gold Rule:
A shooting star on gold is meaningful only when:
- it appears after a bullish push
- it forms at resistance, prior high, R1/R2/R3, upper trendline, or liquidity sweep
- upper wick is large relative to body
- body closes near low
- next candle confirms below the candle low or structure breaks
- price is not mid-range

Gold Mode should not short a shooting star blindly.

Example:
Decision: WAIT / Conditional SELL
Why: Gold printed a shooting star at resistance, but confirmation is required.
Confirmation: Sell thesis strengthens only if price closes below the shooting star low or retests and rejects.
Invalidation: Cancel if gold closes above the wick high.
Risk Note: Gold wicks heavily; do not enter from candle shape alone.

SECTION 6 — Gold-specific risk model

Gold needs a different risk layer.

GoldRiskVerdict:
- atrState: normal / elevated / extreme
- wickRisk: low / medium / high
- spreadRisk: normal / wide / unstable
- newsRisk: low / medium / high
- stopDistanceStatus: too_tight / acceptable / too_wide
- positionSizeAdjustment
- maxScalpRiskSuggestion
- riskWarning

Rules:
- Tight stops should be downgraded during high ATR.
- Spread widening blocks scalp entries.
- High-impact USD news blocks or downgrades unless strategy explicitly allows post-news mode.
- If price is too far from entry trigger, mark too_late.
- If stop must be unrealistically wide, mark poor quality.
- If target room is smaller than required risk/reward, block setup.

Gold stop logic:
- stop beyond wick high/low for rejection setups
- stop beyond sweep extreme for liquidity sweeps
- stop beyond retest failure level for breakout/retest
- stop beyond structure, not arbitrary pips
- use ATR buffer
