- current price

- take profit

- current P&L

- peak profit

- MFE
- MAE
- profit giveback
- trade age
- current AI label
- close urgency score
- continuation score

- reversal risk score
- fakeout risk score
- volatility risk score
- data quality score

- Ask AI
- Review Close
- Set Alert

- Partial Close Review
- Hold and Monitor

2. Sniper Exit Watchlist

Create a “Sniper Exit Watchlist” section that automatically lists open trades needing attention.

Include trades where:
- profit is fading
- price is near stop loss
- price is near take profit
- reversal risk is rising
- fakeout risk is rising
- close urgency is high
- trade is close to break-even after being in profit
- trade has exceeded preferred hold time
- volatility/spread risk is high if data exists

Each card should show:
- trade
- current P&L
- alert reason
- urgency tier
- recommended action
- Review Close button

3. Alert Delivery

Wire trade exit alerts into the app notification system.

Alert types:
- In-app notification
- Push notification if supported
- Assistant notification bubble
- Trade card badge

Alert examples:
- “EURUSD profit is fading. You are still positive, but price has given back 42% from peak.”
- “Gold is near your stop loss. Review risk.”
- “Close urgency is high. Tap to review exit options.”
- “Momentum has weakened. This may be a retracement or possible reversal.”

Do not send repeated spam alerts. Add cooldown logic:
- no duplicate alert for the same trade/reason within a reasonable time window
- allow urgent alerts to override cooldown only when severity increases

5. AI Exit Conversation Memory

When a user asks AI about a trade, save the AI analysis history for that trade.

The trade should have a conversation timeline:
- user asked
- AI answered
- alert fired
- user reviewed close
- user confirmed close
- user ignored alert
- trade later closed
- result after alert

This helps the user see:
- what AI warned about
- whether the warning was helpful
- whether closing would have protected profit
- whether holding was better

- Which of my trades need attention right now?
- Which trade is closest to reversing?

- Should I close anything?

AI must answer from real trade state only. If data is missing, say so.

8. Guardrails

AI must never:
- guarantee maximum profit
- promise a trade will win
- silently close a trade
- bypass backend guards
- hide live risk
- expose another user’s trades
- expose shared master account credentials
- invent candles, P&L, signals, or news data

AI should use language like:
- “Based on available data”
- “This appears to be”
- “This may be”
- “The risk is increasing”
- “Consider reviewing a close”
- “I need more candle/scanner data to judge this accurately”
