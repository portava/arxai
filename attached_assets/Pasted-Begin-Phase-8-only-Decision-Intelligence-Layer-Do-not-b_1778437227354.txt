Rules:
- A winning trade is not automatically a good decision.
- A losing trade is not automatically a bad decision.
- No-trade decisions can be scored as successful.
- Decision quality must consider risk discipline, setup quality, execution quality, trader state, market regime, expected value, survival impact, and rule compliance.
- Conviction controls aggression, size, hold time, and monitoring intensity.
- Strategic Patience can recommend WAIT, MONITOR_ONLY, SOFT_BLOCK, or HARD_BLOCK.
- Future Risk Simulation must test dangerous scenarios before approval.
- Decision Governance must convert decision quality into permission level, aggression level, and execution limits.
- Risk Governor and Control Tower can override Decision Governance.
- All outputs must be logged into the Black Box Vault.
- Keep all previous phase tests passing.
- No unrestricted live auto-execution yet.

Required outputs:
- decisionQualityScore
- expectancyScore
- convictionScore
- patienceScore
- survivalImpactScore
- futureRiskScore
- noTradeQualityScore
- allowedPermissionLevel
- maxAggressionLevel
- maxPositionSize
- requiredConfirmation

Acceptance criteria:
- Bad winning trades can receive low decision scores.
- Good losing trades can receive high decision scores.
- No-trade decisions can be rewarded.
- Conviction affects trade aggression.
- Future risk scenarios can reduce or block approval.
- Decision Governance maps decisions into clear permissions.
- Existing tests still pass.
