Upgrade ARX AI news intelligence so it acts as a true risk filter, including WAIT / AVOID / NO TRADE decisions.

SCOPE LOCK:
Only work on:
1. News-risk scoring.
2. Scanner ranking adjustment.
3. Ruby trading decision context.
4. Trade ticket news-risk warning.
5. Alerts/watch mode.

Do not:
- Enable live trading.
- Place trades.
- Change execution permissions.
- Change kill switches.
- Change admin approvals.
- Rewrite scanner logic from scratch.
- Rewrite Ruby brain architecture.

GOAL:
ARX AI should not always try to find a trade. Sometimes the correct decision is WAIT, WATCH, or AVOID because news risk is too high.

Add these decision states across scanner, Ruby, and trade ticket:

Decision states:
- TRADE WATCH
- WAIT FOR CONFIRMATION
- AVOID FOR NOW
- NO TRADE
- NEWS RISK HIGH
- EVENT RISK ACTIVE
- TECHNICAL SETUP CONFLICTS WITH NEWS

Scanner behavior:
If technicals look good but news risk is high, scanner should downgrade the result.

Examples:
- Strong buy setup + high-impact USD news in 15 minutes = WAIT FOR CONFIRMATION
- Strong sell setup + world leader statement causing sudden volatility = AVOID FOR NOW
- Clean setup + no major news = TRADE WATCH
- Conflicting news/technical direction = NO TRADE or WAIT

Scanner cards should show:
- Technical Score
- Historical/Backtest Score
- News Risk
- Final Trade Read:
  - Watch
  - Wait
  - Avoid
  - No Trade

Ruby behavior:
Ruby must be allowed to say:
- “I would not trade this right now.”
- “The setup is technically clean, but the news risk is too high.”
- “Wait until after the event volatility settles.”
- “This is a watchlist candidate, not an entry yet.”
- “News and technicals conflict, so confidence is low.”

Ruby must not force a buy/sell answer when the safer answer is no trade.

Trade ticket behavior:
Before final confirmation, show a News Risk Gate:
- If news risk is medium: show caution.
- If news risk is high/critical: show strong warning.
- If news risk is critical: require extra confirmation if existing risk rules allow.
- Do not block trades unless existing risk rules already support blocking. If no risk-block rule exists, show a strong warning only.

User-facing wording:
Use clean language:
- “News risk is high. Waiting may be safer.”
- “Major event nearby. Volatility may spike.”
- “Technicals and news are conflicting.”
- “No clean trade right now.”

Do not use raw provider names, route names, function names, or debug labels.

Acceptance checklist:
1. Scanner can return WAIT / AVOID / NO TRADE, not only BUY/SELL.
2. Ruby can recommend no trade when appropriate.
3. Trade ticket shows a news-risk gate before confirmation.
4. News risk downgrades scanner ranking when appropriate.
5. News risk does not fake certainty.
6. News unavailable state is clean.
7. No live trades are placed.
8. No execution permissions, kill switches, or admin approvals are changed.
