Rules:
- Do not label the trader emotionally without evidence.
- Use neutral language like “elevated risk pattern detected,” not “you are emotional.”
- Build a personal baseline before making strong behavior decisions.
- Compare current behavior against the trader’s normal behavior.
- Trader DNA and Cognitive systems cannot place trades.
- They can recommend WAIT, REDUCE_SIZE, COOLDOWN, RECOVERY_MODE, PAPER_MODE, or HARD_BLOCK.
- Risk Governor consumes traderRiskScore and cognitiveRiskScore.
- Control Tower consumes recommendedPermissionLevel.
- Personal Risk Prescription must output specific permission changes, not vague warnings.
- Recovery protocols must define how permissions are restored.
- Temporal analysis must evaluate behavior sequences, not isolated events.
- Contextual analysis must combine behavior with volatility, execution stress, disagreement score, and global state.
- Long-horizon analysis must detect gradual discipline, aggression, and override drift.
- All behavior evidence, prescriptions, recovery outcomes, and drift analysis must be logged into the Black Box Vault.
- Keep all previous phase tests passing.
- No unrestricted live auto-execution yet.
