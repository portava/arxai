## Limits

- The gain is small: +0.8% of the interval score pooled. It holds at the pooled lower bound, but only 7 of 28 series clear zero on their own and 8 are worse. It is a modestly better-sized range, not a different kind of forecast.
- Tested on M15 and H1 only, so only those bar sizes use it (the 8h/12h presets on M15 and the 2d preset on H1). M1, M5, M30 and H4 keep the 120-bar band: they were not measured.
- Production fits the hour-of-week factors and the scale once per model, on the bars that closed by the model's `trainedThrough`, and computes the EWMA over the feed the forecast reads (up to 5000 bars). The research refitted before each of 6 blocks on everything earlier. Same estimator, coarser refit schedule.
- The range engine choice (history vs volatility) is now made against this band on the model's selection and gate setups, which the profile was not fitted on.
- The unscaled diagnostic scored higher (+1.3%), giving narrower bands. It was not the pre-registered variant, and switching to it after seeing these numbers would pick on the test data, so it is not used.
- Range width only. It does not touch the probabilities, the paths' middle or any direction claim.
