# Smart Chart candle catalogue: explicit geometry, first expansion

This document records the first expansion checkpoint. The current successor's additional definitions and criteria are documented in `SMART_CHART_CANDLE_CATALOGUE_V2.md`; its exact current coverage is in `SMART_CHART_CANDLE_COVERAGE.json`. The measurements below remain the original checkpoint's historical evidence.

This checkpoint adds a pure shared candle catalogue to the existing source-bound Smart Chart model. It contains 34 named variants grouped into 10 geometry families. Together with the existing Shooting Star and two Engulfing implementations, 37 of the owner's 104 named candle entries have code and focused fixtures. This is named-requirement coverage, not 37 independent signals, a completion percentage for the application, or proof of forecasting value. The other 67 entries remain in `SMART_CHART_CANDLE_COVERAGE.json`; no owner requirement is excluded.

The module exports `CANDLE_DEFINITIONS`, version `arx-candle-geometry-v1`, and `scanCandlestickCatalogue`. Definitions include names, exact criteria, family grouping and primary references. Existing API pattern engines remain unchanged. The frontend invokes the catalogue only after its established source, opaque identity, symbol, timeframe and closed-bar boundary checks. Every new result is an **observed shape**, with actual prices and observation time. No confidence percentage, future confirmation, inferred trade instruction or execution authority is added.

## Primary research and chosen conventions

References were checked on 2026-10-26. TradingView's [automatic candle detection](https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000584462-automatic-candlestick-pattern-detection/) and [pattern catalogue announcement](https://www.tradingview.com/blog/en/new-candlestick-patterns-20461/) establish the comparison feature: automatic chart labels with explanatory details and configurable patterns. They do not establish ARX accuracy or speed parity. ARX keeps one focused drawing and at most three summary families; basic body facts remain in details. This checkpoint changes the pure model; parent integration owns actual embedded presentation/browser validation.

The [TA-Lib function catalogue](https://ta-lib.org/functions/) and the implementation comments/conditions linked below provide reproducible reference definitions. This is an independent TypeScript implementation with explicit ARX thresholds, **not TA-Lib numerical equivalence**. TA-Lib generally uses configurable candle-setting averages; TradingView offers several trend-filter choices. Different choices legitimately produce different detections and must not be presented as identical results.

| Family | ARX definition and material reference differences |
| --- | --- |
| Basic body | Up/down follows close versus open. Long is above the prior ten mean body sizes; short is positive and below half that mean. These are body facts, not all the shadow conditions of [TA-Lib Long Line](https://github.com/TA-Lib/ta-lib/blob/main/src/ta_func/ta_CDLLONGLINE.c) or [Short Line](https://github.com/TA-Lib/ta-lib/blob/main/src/ta_func/ta_CDLSHORTLINE.c). |
| Indecision | Non-Doji body at most 30% of range, with both shadows longer than body. High Wave further requires both at least twice body and range at least 1.5 times prior mean. References: [Spinning Top](https://github.com/TA-Lib/ta-lib/blob/main/src/ta_func/ta_CDLSPINNINGTOP.c), [High Wave](https://github.com/TA-Lib/ta-lib/blob/main/src/ta_func/ta_CDLHIGHWAVE.c). |
| Marubozu | Long body and each shadow at most 5% of current range. Opening/Closing variants require only the named side small, with the other side larger; full Marubozu is not counted again as either variant. References: [Marubozu](https://github.com/TA-Lib/ta-lib/blob/main/src/ta_func/ta_CDLMARUBOZU.c), [Closing Marubozu](https://github.com/TA-Lib/ta-lib/blob/main/src/ta_func/ta_CDLCLOSINGMARUBOZU.c). |
| Doji | Body at most 10% of nonzero current range. Dragonfly/Gravestone use 5% short-shadow and 60% long-shadow limits. Four-Price is exact equal OHLC, shown only as a neutral zero-range fact. [TradingView Doji](https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000583767-doji/), [Dragonfly](https://github.com/TA-Lib/ta-lib/blob/main/src/ta_func/ta_CDLDRAGONFLYDOJI.c), [Gravestone](https://github.com/TA-Lib/ta-lib/blob/main/src/ta_func/ta_CDLGRAVESTONEDOJI.c). |
| Doji subtypes | Long-Legged requires **both** shadows at least 30% of range and a range at least 1.5 times prior mean. This is stricter than [TA-Lib's one-or-two-long-shadows condition](https://github.com/TA-Lib/ta-lib/blob/main/src/ta_func/ta_CDLLONGLEGGEDDOJI.c). [Rickshaw Man](https://github.com/TA-Lib/ta-lib/blob/main/src/ta_func/ta_CDLRICKSHAWMAN.c) additionally places the body midpoint within 10% of range midpoint. [Takuri](https://github.com/TA-Lib/ta-lib/blob/main/src/ta_func/ta_CDLTAKURI.c) is a Dragonfly subtype after a decline with lower shadow at least three prior mean bodies and range at least 1.5 prior mean ranges. These share one Doji summary slot. |
| Hammer family | Non-Doji body at most 30% of range, principal shadow at least twice body, other shadow at most 10% of range. [Hammer](https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000583775-hammer-bullish/) follows a decline; [Hanging Man](https://github.com/TA-Lib/ta-lib/blob/main/src/ta_func/ta_CDLHANGINGMAN.c) follows a rise. Inverted Hammer follows a decline; unlike [TA-Lib's variant](https://github.com/TA-Lib/ta-lib/blob/main/src/ta_func/ta_CDLINVERTEDHAMMER.c), this ARX version does not require a body gap. |
| Belt Hold | Long body, small opening-side shadow, opposite preceding trend. [TA-Lib Belt Hold](https://github.com/TA-Lib/ta-lib/blob/main/src/ta_func/ta_CDLBELTHOLD.c) supplies the geometry; ARX adds the explicit preceding-trend check. |
| Harami | Long first body and opposite-colour second body no more than half its size, strictly inside both body edges, after the relevant trend. A Doji second candle is the Cross variant only. References: [TradingView Harami](https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000583777-harami-bearish/), [TA-Lib Harami Cross](https://github.com/TA-Lib/ta-lib/blob/main/src/ta_func/ta_CDLHARAMICROSS.c). ARX requires strict containment rather than partial edge matching. Shadows may extend beyond the first body. |
| Piercing / Dark Cloud | Two opposite long bodies after the appropriate trend; second open beyond first low/high and close strictly past the first body's midpoint, still inside its body. [Piercing](https://github.com/TA-Lib/ta-lib/blob/main/src/ta_func/ta_CDLPIERCING.c), [Dark Cloud](https://github.com/TA-Lib/ta-lib/blob/main/src/ta_func/ta_CDLDARKCLOUDCOVER.c). Requiring the second Dark Cloud body to be long is an additional ARX restriction. |
| Tweezers | Long trend-following first body, opposite-colour second candle, matching lows/highs within 5% of the prior mean range. [TradingView's Tweezer Bottom](https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000592709-tweezer-bottom-bullish/) supplies the paired outline and trend context; its qualitative near-equality is made explicit here. Equal levels do not prove support/resistance will hold. |
| Morning / Evening Stars | Long first body; middle body at most 30% of it and entirely body-gapped beyond it; long opposite third body closes past the first midpoint and opens beyond the middle body. Doji middle bodies use distinct Doji-Star names. [Morning Star](https://github.com/TA-Lib/ta-lib/blob/main/src/ta_func/ta_CDLMORNINGSTAR.c), [Evening Star](https://github.com/TA-Lib/ta-lib/blob/main/src/ta_func/ta_CDLEVENINGSTAR.c), [Morning Doji Star](https://github.com/TA-Lib/ta-lib/blob/main/src/ta_func/ta_CDLMORNINGDOJISTAR.c), [Evening Doji Star](https://github.com/TA-Lib/ta-lib/blob/main/src/ta_func/ta_CDLEVENINGDOJISTAR.c). ARX's 50% penetration, long third body and third opening gap are stricter than TA-Lib defaults. Full-range gaps are not required, so these are not Abandoned Baby implementations. |

ARX prior trend means five prior contiguous closes: at least three of the four changes agree, and the net move is at least half their mean high-low range. The evaluated pattern's candles are excluded. This conservative short-window definition is not TradingView SMA50/SMA200 equivalence. Long/short baselines use ten prior candles, also excluding the candle being classified. No positive baseline means no relative-length claim. Numerical comparisons use a relative `1e-12` rounding tolerance; it is neither a tick size nor a spread estimate.

## Evidence, causality and coverage limits

- Inputs retain actual source/symbol/timeframe/opaque identity at the chart binding boundary. No prior-source observations are reused. The pure catalogue also rejects nonfinite or incoherent OHLC, mismatched interval lengths, duplicate/nonchronological times, future finalized closes and contradictory finality.
- Explicitly forming bars are not pattern evidence. Future replay rows cannot change an earlier prefix. New observations become available only at the final pattern candle's close; no pivot or event is backdated.
- Multi-candle patterns require adjacent interval timestamps. Prior-trend checks refuse gaps as well. There is no session-calendar inference in this slice, so some valid patterns around market closures are intentionally withheld. Missing observations are never synthesized.
- Analysis retains the existing 600-bar cap and latest 12 event endpoints. The model reports available/analyzed counts and truncation; relative definitions may need ten prior observations. This is recent-window analysis, not exhaustive all-history discovery.
- The initial core's Shooting Star/Engulfing lifecycle and chart-pattern engine are preserved. New shapes use `observed`, with no invented subsequent confirmation or invalidation. A shape can remain a historical observation even if later prices move against its conventional interpretation.
- Several Doji, body and Marubozu entries describe the same bar at different specificity. Family grouping prevents their names from appearing as multiple independent confirmations in summary. Basic up/down/long/short and Four-Price facts are detail-only.

The coverage inventory preserves all 104 owner names. Ambiguous or uncommon names such as Northern/Southern Doji, Turn Up/Down, Last Engulfing and some mirrored variants are explicitly pending definition research. Remaining recognizable families also remain pending implementation/validation. No placeholder algorithm is advertised as implemented.
