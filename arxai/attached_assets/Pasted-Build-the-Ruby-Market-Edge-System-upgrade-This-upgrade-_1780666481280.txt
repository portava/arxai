Build the Ruby Market Edge System upgrade.

This upgrade is focused on making Ruby faster, smarter, more specific, more trustworthy, and more useful inside the scanner, Smart Chart, trade preview, live trade monitoring, and future investor explanations.

Current problems:
1. Ruby sometimes sends signals too late after the move has already happened.
2. Ruby does not spot forming trends early enough.
3. Ruby scanner explanations are too vague and generic.
4. Users need to know why a market is bullish or bearish.
5. Users need to know what happened, whether news/economic conditions affected the trade, whether the signal is early/ready/late, and what invalidates the idea.
6. Ruby needs to calculate execution cost, spread, slippage, lot-size impact, and approximate starting drawdown/upside before users enter.
7. News alerts and economic events should appear directly on the Smart Chart before they affect the trade.
8. Ruby needs to manage and explain trades after entry, not only before entry.
9. Ruby needs to learn from late signals, missed moves, slippage, wins, losses, and user behavior.

This is an intelligence and UI/analysis upgrade.
Do not change the live execution path.
Do not place live trades for this build.
Do not add Paper Trading, simulator, mock, fake, sandbox, or test-trading language.
Do not expose backend route names, table names, raw JSON, stack traces, code terms, or developer wording to users.
Preserve existing scanner, Ruby, Smart Chart, live bridge, trade modal, retcode, admin/user role, investor role, and permission wiring.

PART 3 — RUBY SIGNAL LIFECYCLE STATES
====================================================

Ruby should not only output Buy/Sell/No Trade.

Every market/opportunity should move through lifecycle states:

1. Watching
Meaning:
- Market is being monitored.
- No clear setup yet.
User-facing:
“Ruby is watching this market. No clean setup yet.”

2. Trend Forming
Meaning:
- Early directional pressure is building.
- Not ready for trade.
User-facing:
“Trend pressure is forming, but Ruby needs more confirmation.”

3. Setup Forming
Meaning:
- Market is approaching a zone or structure needed for entry.
- Watch zone should appear.
User-facing:
“Setup forming. Wait for price to reach the watch zone.”

4. Entry Approaching
Meaning:
- Price is close to ideal entry area.
- User should prepare, not chase.
User-facing:
“Entry may be approaching. Ruby is watching confirmation.”

5. Entry Window Open
Meaning:
- Setup is active and fresh.
- Trade can be reviewed if risk/execution are valid.
User-facing:
“Entry window open. Review setup now.”

6. Late — Do Not Chase
Meaning:
- Move already traveled too far.
- Remaining reward is not worth risk.
User-facing:
“Late — do not chase. Wait for pullback or new setup.”

7. Invalid
Meaning:
- Setup broke.
- Bias may need reset.
User-facing:
“Setup invalidated. Ruby is no longer using this idea.”

8. Expired
Meaning:
- Signal timed out or became stale.
User-facing:
“Signal expired. Ruby needs fresh data.”

Use these states in:
- Focus Scan
- Broad Scan
- Smart Chart
- Ruby Scalp Signals
- trade preview
- alerting system
- admin signal logs

PART 8 — NEWS BEHAVIOR RULES
====================================================

Ruby must behave differently around news.

Before high-impact news:
- downgrade normal scalp signals
- show watch zones instead of aggressive entries
- warn user about volatility/spread/slippage
- avoid market-order recommendations unless setup is extremely clean and risk is defined

During news:
- mark volatility spike active
- avoid first reaction candle
- warn about spread/slippage
- mark market unstable if needed

After news:
- watch first spike
- detect fakeout/reversal/continuation
- wait for retest or confirmation
- wait for spread normalization
- resume signals only when volatility cools or structure confirms

Admin settings:
- no scalp signals X minutes before high-impact news
- no market orders X minutes before news
- watch mode only during high-impact news
- resume after volatility normalizes
- post-news wait window
- affected symbols/currencies settings
- severity threshold for popups

User-facing examples:
“News risk is high. Ruby is watching zones, not recommending a market entry.”
“The first news spike is active. Avoid chasing the first candle.”
“Post-news direction is not confirmed yet. Ruby is waiting for a retest.”
