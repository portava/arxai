Goal:
Every open trade should have a clear AI-generated exit plan based on the user’s trading style, trade timeframe, risk, current P&L, market behavior, and live trade conditions.

Important:
Do not guarantee maximum profit.
Do not auto-close trades.
Do not silently move stops.
Do not bypass backend guards.
Do not expose secrets.
All live actions require explicit confirmation.

PART 1 — Smart Exit Plan

For every open trade, generate an Exit Plan with:

1. Primary exit idea
2. Conservative exit level
3. Aggressive exit level
4. Protect-profit level
5. Invalidation level
6. Partial-close suggestion if available
7. Stop-move suggestion if available
8. Trailing-stop suggestion if available
9. Time-based exit warning
10. What would make the AI change its opinion

Exit plan should answer:
- Where should I consider closing?
- Where should I protect profit?
- Where is the trade invalidated?
- What level proves continuation?
- What level suggests fakeout/reversal?
- When does holding become risky?
- Should I take partial profit?
- Should I move stop loss to break-even?
- Should I trail stop?

PART 2 — Time-Based Sniper Close Logic

Add time horizon support.

Users should be able to choose:
- scalp: 1–15 minutes
- short intraday: 15–60 minutes
- intraday: 1–6 hours
- swing: multi-day
- custom hold time

The AI should track:
- trade age
- how long the trade has been profitable
- how long the trade has been stalled
- how long momentum has weakened
- whether price has failed to continue within expected time
- whether the trade is near end of user’s intended hold window

Add alerts:

- “Profit has not expanded after your expected window.”
