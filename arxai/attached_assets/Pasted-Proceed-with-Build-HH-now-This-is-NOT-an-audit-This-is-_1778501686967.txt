Build HH = Risk Governor + Trader Readiness System.

Goal:
Create a central Risk Governor that evaluates trader readiness, system readiness, paper trading safety, AI performance quality, autopilot safety, risk exposure, and learning maturity.

The Risk Governor should answer:
- Is the system safe to continue paper trading?
- Should autopilot keep running or pause?
- Is the trader overtrading?
- Is the AI learning enough before scaling?
- Are losses clustering?
- Are mistakes repeating?
- Is market data reliable?
- Is the paper strategy mature enough?
- Should the system go into WATCH_ONLY, PAPER_ALLOWED, PAPER_PAUSED, or LOCKED mode?

3. Readiness scoring model

Create a readiness score from 0–100.

Suggested scoring categories:

A. Risk discipline, 25 points
- respects max daily paper loss
- no revenge-trade cooldown violations
- no overtrading
- no excessive same-symbol exposure
- no repeated high-risk trades

B. Strategy edge maturity, 25 points
- enough paper trade sample size
- positive or stable strategy edge
- consistent symbol performance
- no destructive drawdown pattern
- learning confidence improving

C. AI decision quality, 20 points
- AA decisions are not reckless
- confidence calibration is improving
- risk scores are controlled
- decision-to-trade rate is reasonable
- decision-to-win rate is trackable

D. System reliability, 15 points
- DD market data quality is good
- no stale-data pattern
- no frequent provider failures
- FF autopilot cycles are stable
- EE paper execution is idempotent

E. Learning/debrief quality, 15 points
- BB debriefs are created
- CC learning payloads process once
- mistake patterns are tracked
- repeated mistakes are decreasing
- lessons are actionable

Map score to grade:
- A = 90–100
- B = 80–89
- C = 70–79
- D = 60–69
- F = below 60

4. Hard block rules

Risk Governor must return WATCH_ONLY or LOCKED if any hard block is active.

Hard blocks should include:
- live trading attempted or enabled
- canPlaceTrades is true
- MARKET_DATA_MODE is not read_only
- daily paper loss limit exceeded
- max open paper trades exceeded
- repeated same-symbol conflict
- extreme market data degradation
- autopilot error spike
- duplicate execution bug detected
- missing safety configuration
- Build AA unavailable
- Build EE unavailable
- paper execution trying to call live broker functions

5. Paper pause rules

Risk Governor should return PAPER_PAUSED if:
- daily loss limit is close to being hit
- multiple losses happen back-to-back
- revenge cooldown is active
- repeated mistake pattern is rising
- market data is degraded
- autopilot errors are elevated
- risk scores are trending too high
- learning confidence is too low for expansion

6. Paper caution rules

Risk Governor should return PAPER_CAUTION if:
- system is mostly safe but sample size is small
- win rate is unstable
- strategy edge is not mature
- learning confidence is LOW
- market data is fallback-only
- mistakes are present but not critical

7. Paper allowed rules

Risk Governor may return PAPER_ALLOWED only if:
- no hard blocks exist
- daily paper loss limit is not hit
- open paper trades are within limits
- market data is usable
- autopilot errors are low
- risk scores are controlled
- BB/CC/GG systems are functioning
- learning confidence is at least acceptable
