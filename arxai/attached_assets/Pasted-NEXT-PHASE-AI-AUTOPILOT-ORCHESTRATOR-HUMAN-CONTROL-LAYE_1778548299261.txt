==================================================
1. CREATE AI AUTOPILOT CONTROL CENTER
==================================================

Create page:

/autopilot-control-center

This page should control all AI automation.

Modes:

- OFF
- OBSERVE_ONLY
- AI_ASSIST
- DEMO_AUTO_SIMULATOR
- LIVE_INTENT_AUTO_TESTER
- FUTURE_MT5_LIVE_AUTO_LOCKED

Mode meanings:

OFF:
- AI does nothing.

OBSERVE_ONLY:
- AI scans markets and logs observations.
- No trade cards.
- No simulated orders.
- No live intents.

AI_ASSIST:
- AI generates trade cards.
- User must approve.
- No auto execution.

DEMO_AUTO_SIMULATOR:
- AI can auto-create simulator trades after risk approval.
- No real broker execution.

LIVE_INTENT_AUTO_TESTER:
- AI can generate live-style intents.
- MT5 not connected means intents are saved as PENDING_MT5_CONNECTION.
- No real broker execution.

FUTURE_MT5_LIVE_AUTO_LOCKED:
- visible but locked until MT5 is connected, verified, risk approved, and manually armed.

5. CREATE AUTOPILOT DECISION PIPELINE
==================================================

Every autopilot decision must follow this exact pipeline:

1. Get current market data.
2. Check data quality.
3. Check session clock.
4. Check news risk.
5. Run market scanner.
6. Rank opportunities.
7. Match opportunity to selected strategy.
8. Generate AI trade card.
9. Calculate entry sniper score.
10. Calculate opportunity score.
11. Grade trade.
12. Run Risk Governor 2.0.
13. Check OMS duplicate/idempotency.
14. Decide:
   - WAIT
   - REJECT
   - ASK_USER_APPROVAL
   - SUBMIT_SIMULATOR
   - CREATE_LIVE_INTENT_PENDING_MT5
15. Log decision.
16. Update decision stream.
17. Update journal/calendar if action taken.
18. Feed outcome into learning loop.

No shortcuts.
