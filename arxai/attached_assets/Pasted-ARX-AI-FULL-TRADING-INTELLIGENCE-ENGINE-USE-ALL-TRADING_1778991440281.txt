PHASE 4 — NEWS + CURRENT EVENTS RULES
==================================================

Add strict rules for news/current events.

ARX AI must never invent news.

For every news/current event used in a trading answer, store/show:

- headline or short summary
- source/provider
- publishedAt
- fetchedAt
- impacted symbol/asset/currency if available
- relevance reason
- confidence/impact level
- whether it is confirmed or unverified

If no real news source is configured, ARX AI must say:

“News/current events data is unavailable because no live news provider is connected.”

If news is stale, ARX AI must say:

“News data is stale and should not be treated as live.”

If news is unrelated to the symbol/setup, do not use it as a reason.

If current events are broad or uncertain, treat them as risk context, not proof.

Do not allow AI to say:
- “News caused this move” unless there is actual relevant news data.
- “Current events confirm this trade” unless supported by real source data.
- “This will move because of news” as certainty.

Use safer language:
- “Relevant news may be influencing volatility.”
- “There is not enough news data to confirm the cause.”
- “This setup carries event-risk because of the scheduled release.”

PHASE 9 — SNIPER SETUP REQUIREMENTS
==================================================

Sniper setups must be stricter.

A Sniper setup should require:

1. Strong entry location
2. Tight invalidation
3. Clear liquidity or structure reason
4. Strong risk/reward
5. Multi-timeframe agreement or clear lower-timeframe tactical setup
6. TP levels
7. Historical level/context support
8. News/current events risk check
9. Reversal/fakeout warning
10. Time sensitivity
11. Spread/volatility check
12. No major missing data that would make the setup unsafe

If sniper conditions are not met, AI should say:

“This is not a sniper setup. It may be a regular setup, wait setup, or avoid.”

If news/current events are unavailable, do not label the setup “fully confirmed.”

PHASE 11 — ASSUMPTION CONTROL
==================================================

Any trading assumption must be verified before being stated.

Do not say:

- “This is reversing”
- “This is continuation”
- “Liquidity was swept”
- “News caused this”
- “Current events caused this”
- “Trend is bullish”
- “Support will hold”
- “This is safe”

Unless the available tools, history, and news support it.

Instead say:

- “Available structure suggests…”
- “Historical behavior around this level suggests…”
- “Momentum currently leans…”
- “News data is unavailable, so I cannot confirm a news driver.”
- “This looks like a possible fakeout, but confirmation is incomplete.”

Add an assumption checker that flags:

- unsupported assumption
- stale data assumption
- missing market data assumption
- missing historical data assumption
- missing news data assumption
- missing current-events data assumption
- missing volume assumption
- conflicting signal assumption
