PHASE 5 — FIXING RULES

After the audit identifies failures, fix them carefully.

Rules:

1. Preserve working wiring.
2. Do not remove features unless clearly dead, duplicate, or unused and documented.
3. Do not redesign the whole app during audit unless required to fix broken behavior.
4. Do not expose backend/internal wording to users.
5. Do not fake pass anything.
6. Do not use frontend-only success for backend-critical actions.
7. Do not mark live trade passed unless MT5/EA/broker result confirms it.
8. Fix the root cause, not the symptom.
9. After each fix, rerun the failed test and connected ecosystem tests.
10. If a fix risks breaking another system, choose a safer adapter/wrapper approach instead.
11. Preserve routes, hooks, services, state stores, permissions, MT5 bridge paths, scanner paths, Ruby paths, realtime update paths, feed paths, and candle-history paths unless you prove they are broken and replace them with a verified equivalent.
12. Do not bypass existing wiring to make a test pass. If existing wiring is wrong, repair the chain properly.
13. If a UI feature is not wired, either wire it fully, hide it, or convert it into a truthful empty state.
14. If a backend function is not involved with the ecosystem, document it and classify it as dormant, future-use, duplicate, or remove candidate.
15. Every fix must include a retest showing the full wiring chain works.
16. For scanner/feed/candle fixes, do not make chart, Ruby, and scanner use separate conflicting data truth. Prefer one normalized market data truth contract.
17. For floating errors, do not simply hide the error unless it is redundant and the underlying root cause is fixed or represented by a better user-safe state.
18. For candle history, do not generate fake candles to cover missing data. Fix retrieval/backfill/pagination or show honest provider limitation.
