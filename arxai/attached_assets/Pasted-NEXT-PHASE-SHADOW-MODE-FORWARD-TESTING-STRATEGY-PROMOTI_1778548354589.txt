NEXT PHASE: SHADOW MODE + FORWARD TESTING + STRATEGY PROMOTION GATES

Goal:
Build a professional forward-testing layer where the AI can run in shadow mode, make live-style decisions, track outcomes, rank strategies, and decide which strategies deserve promotion.

The app already has:
- full tester access
- live chart
- market simulator
- strategy lab
- backtesting
- market scanner
- command room
- OMS
- position manager
- P/L engine
- Risk Governor 2.0
- AI Autopilot Orchestrator
- MT5 deferred

Now build:
- Shadow Mode
- Forward Testing
- Strategy Tournament
- Confidence Calibration
- Promotion/Demotion Gates
- AI Readiness Score

Important:
- Do not fake MT5 connection.
- Do not place real broker orders.
- Do not claim broker fills happened.
- Keep full tester access open.
- Keep real broker execution deferred until MT5 is connected later.
- Shadow mode decisions are observations, not real trades.

==================================================
1. CREATE SHADOW MODE
==================================================

Create page:

/shadow-mode

Shadow Mode means:
- AI scans market conditions
- AI generates trade ideas
- AI creates hypothetical entries/exits
- AI tracks what would have happened
- AI does not submit simulator orders unless user chooses
- AI does not create broker orders
- AI does not claim execution

Shadow decision statuses:

- SHADOW_OBSERVATION
- SHADOW_TRADE_IDEA
- SHADOW_WAIT
- SHADOW_REJECTED
- SHADOW_TRACKING_OUTCOME
- SHADOW_WIN
- SHADOW_LOSS
- SHADOW_BREAKEVEN
- SHADOW_EXPIRED

Each shadow decision should include:

- symbol
- timeframe
- strategy
- market condition
- recommended action
- entry
- stop loss
- take profit
- confidence score
- opportunity score
- entry sniper score
- trade grade
- risk governor result
- reason for decision
- reason to avoid
- outcome after tracking period

==================================================
5. CREATE STRATEGY PROMOTION GATES
==================================================

Create page:

/strategy-promotion

Strategies should only be promoted if they pass rules.

Promotion levels:

- TESTING
- WATCHLIST
- PAPER_APPROVED
- DEMO_APPROVED
- LIVE_INTENT_APPROVED
- FUTURE_MT5_LIVE_LOCKED

Promotion requirements:

PAPER_APPROVED:
- minimum 20 shadow decisions
- win rate above configured threshold
- average R positive
- max drawdown acceptable
- no risk governor violations

DEMO_APPROVED:
- minimum 50 forward-test decisions
- positive expectancy
- drawdown under limit
- confidence calibration acceptable
- no overtrading behavior

LIVE_INTENT_APPROVED:
- minimum 100 tested decisions
- positive expectancy
- stable confidence calibration
- risk discipline score above threshold
- no hard risk violations
- user/admin approval

FUTURE_MT5_LIVE_LOCKED:
- visible but locked
- requires MT5 connection
- requires real broker readiness
- requires manual arming later

Do not allow any strategy to jump directly to live.

9. CREATE STRATEGY DEMOTION RULES
==================================================

Demote strategies if:

- win rate drops below threshold
- average R turns negative
- drawdown exceeds limit
- overtrading detected
- repeated risk governor blocks
- confidence calibration fails
- too many low-grade trades
- poor performance during current session

Demotion levels:

- WATCHLIST
- NEEDS_REVIEW
- PAUSED
- RETIRED

User can manually override, but override must be logged.
