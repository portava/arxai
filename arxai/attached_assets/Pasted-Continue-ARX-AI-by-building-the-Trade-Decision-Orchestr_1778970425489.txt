- Should the user hold?

- Should the user move stop loss?
- Should the user trail stop?

PART 2 — Decision Rules

Create clear rule logic.

Examples:

If trade is profitable and profit giveback is high:
- decision_label: Protect profit
- action: REVIEW_MOVE_STOP or REVIEW_PARTIAL_CLOSE

If reversal risk is high and price breaks invalidation level:
- decision_label: Trade invalidated
- action: REVIEW_FULL_CLOSE

If continuation score is high and pullback is healthy:
- decision_label: Healthy pullback
- action: HOLD or WATCH_CLOSELY

If fakeout risk rises after breakout failure:
- decision_label: Exit risk rising
- action: REVIEW_PARTIAL_CLOSE or REVIEW_FULL_CLOSE

If trade is near TP and momentum is weakening:
- decision_label: Protect profit
- action: REVIEW_PARTIAL_CLOSE or REVIEW_FULL_CLOSE

If data is stale:
- decision_label: Data insufficient
- action: NO_ACTION_DATA_INSUFFICIENT

Do not use hardcoded fake market assumptions. Use only available data.

- urgency score jumps significantly
- confidence score drops
- data quality becomes stale
- ignored alert becomes more severe

Avoid spam:
- no duplicate alert for same decision/reason within cooldown
- escalate only when severity increases or decision changes
