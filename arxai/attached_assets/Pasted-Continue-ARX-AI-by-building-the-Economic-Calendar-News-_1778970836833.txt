Continue ARX AI by building the Economic Calendar, News Risk, and Session Context Engine.

Do not rebuild blindly. Inspect the current:
- Market Context Engine
- Risk Governor
- Broker Symbol Specs Engine
- Live Trade Intelligence
- Smart Exit Plan Engine

- Sniper Exit Alerts
- Trade Action Center
- AI assistant context builder
- scanner / market data integrations
- Finnhub or existing market data provider setup
- notification system

- user alert preferences

Goal:
Give ARX AI event-risk awareness so it can warn users before, during, and after high-impact events, and explain when a trade may be moving because of news, session volatility, liquidity, spread, or market timing.

Important:
Do not fake news or calendar data.
Do not invent event times, impact levels, or headlines.
If no provider is connected, return “economic/news data unavailable.”
Do not auto-close trades.
Do not auto-open trades.
Do not bypass backend guards.
Do not expose API keys, bridge tokens, secrets, or MT5 credentials.
Live trade actions still require explicit confirmation.

PART 1 — Economic Calendar Service

Create an economic calendar service that can ingest calendar events from available providers.

Use existing configured providers first.
If no provider is available, build a provider interface/stub and mark status as unavailable.

For each event, store:
- event id
- title
- country/currency
- impact level: low / medium / high / unknown
- event time
- actual value if available
- forecast value if available
- previous value if available
- source
- data freshness
- created_at
- updated_at

Map events to trade symbols:
- EURUSD should care about EUR and USD events.
- GBPUSD should care about GBP and USD events.
- USDJPY should care about USD and JPY events.
- XAUUSD / gold should care heavily about USD events.
- US indices should care about USD / US events.
- Crypto should show news risk only if provider data exists.

PART 2 — News Risk Service

Create a news risk layer.

It should:
- collect relevant market/news items from available provider if configured
- map news to symbols where possible
- detect high-risk language only if real news text exists
- mark news data unavailable when no provider exists
- avoid making up headlines or catalysts

News risk labels:
- No news data
- Low news risk
- Medium news risk
- High news risk
- Unknown news risk

PART 3 — Session Context Engine

Add trading session awareness.

Detect:
- Asian session

- market close window

- low-liquidity period
- high-volatility session window

Use this to label trade context:

- Spread widening risk
- News window risk
- Session transition risk

PART 4 — Trade-Level Event Risk

For each open trade, calculate event risk.

Use:
- symbol
- currencies involved
- account type demo/live
- routing mode
- open time
- current time
- upcoming events
- recent events
- current spread if available
- volatility if available
- trade P&L
- stop loss distance
- take profit distance

Create scores:
- upcomingEventRiskScore
- activeNewsRiskScore
- sessionVolatilityScore
- spreadRiskScore
- liquidityRiskScore
- eventCloseUrgencyScore

Create labels:
- No event risk detected
- Upcoming high-impact event
- Active news window
- Post-news volatility
- Spread risk rising
- Liquidity risk
- Avoid new entries
- Manage open trade carefully
- Close review recommended

PART 5 — Alerts

Create event-risk alerts:

Examples:
- “High-impact USD news in 15 minutes. Review open USD trades.”
- “EURUSD is open during a news-risk window. Spread/volatility may increase.”
- “Gold trade is exposed to upcoming USD event risk.”
- “Market is entering low-liquidity conditions. Protect profit if needed.”
- “News window active. Avoid opening new trades unless you accept the risk.”

Alert actions:
- Ask AI
- Review Close
- Move Stop Review
- Hold and Monitor
- Disable new entries until event passes if risk rules require it

No instant execution.

PART 6 — Risk Governor Integration

Update Risk Governor so event/session risk can affect trade validation.

Admin/user configurable options:
- block new trades before high-impact events
- warn only before high-impact events
- allow closes during news
- allow risk-reducing actions during news
- block opening trades during low-liquidity windows
- block opening trades when spread risk is high
- require extra live confirmation during high-impact news

Important:
Even if new trades are blocked, users should still be able to close or reduce risk unless emergency stop blocks all execution.
