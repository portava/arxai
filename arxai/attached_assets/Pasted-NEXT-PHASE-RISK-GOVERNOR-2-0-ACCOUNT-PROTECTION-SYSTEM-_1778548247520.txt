NEXT PHASE: RISK GOVERNOR 2.0 + ACCOUNT PROTECTION SYSTEM

Goal:
Upgrade the trading app’s risk system into a professional account protection layer.

The app already has:
- full tester access
- live chart
- simulator market engine
- strategy lab
- backtesting
- market scanner
- command room
- market data layer
- OMS
- position manager
- P/L engine
- MT5 deferred

Now build the protection layer that controls:
- max daily loss
- max weekly loss
- max trade risk
- drawdown
- exposure
- overtrading
- revenge trading
- news risk
- spread risk
- volatility risk
- correlated trades
- AI auto-trading limits
- future MT5 live execution safety

Important:
Do not fake MT5 connection.
Do not place real orders.
Do not claim broker execution is available.
Keep full tester access open.
Do not hide pages.
Risk should control execution decisions, not navigation access.

==================================================
9. CONNECT RISK GOVERNOR TO OMS
==================================================

Every order must flow:

1. Create draft order
2. Run Risk Governor 2.0
3. If approved, submit to simulator or live intent queue
4. If rejected, store as RISK_REJECTED
5. Log risk event
6. Update audit log
7. Update decision stream
8. Update journal if needed

Do not allow simulated fills unless risk approves.

For live tester intent:
- If risk approves, save as PENDING_MT5_CONNECTION
- If risk rejects, save as REJECTED_BY_RISK
- Do not place broker order

==================================================
10. CONNECT RISK GOVERNOR TO AI
==================================================

AI must receive risk feedback.

For every AI idea:

- show risk result
- show hard blocks
- show warnings
- show required changes
- explain why AI should wait or reject
- store whether AI respected risk governor

AI should learn:

- which setups are rejected most
- which symbols create risk problems
- whether confidence scores are too aggressive
- whether entry quality is improving
- whether overtrading risk is increasing
