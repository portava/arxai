Goal:
Upgrade ARX pattern recognition so Ruby, Scanner, Scalp Builder, and future Auto Bot can detect chart patterns with measurable rules, confidence, confirmation/invalidation levels, and historical performance tracking.

Important:
Pattern recognition is decision-support only.
Patterns may explain, confirm, warn, rank, or downgrade.
Patterns must never bypass feed truth, Scanner Truth, Trade Health, risk gates, or live execution safety.

- not just "bearish" or "bullish"

Pattern rules:
1. Forming pattern:
   - max confidence 60
   - cannot say "confirmed"
   - cannot produce "ready now"
   - must show the confirmation level

2. Confirmed pattern:
   - requires trigger/neckline/breakout/retest rule
   - can increase confidence only if feed/readiness/risk also allow it

3. Failed pattern:
   - must downgrade Scanner/Ruby read
   - must explain the failure

4. Exhausted pattern:
   - must classify as too-late/chase risk
   - must not produce "valid now"

5. Pattern on degraded feed:
   - can display as historical/context only
   - cannot show live-ready
   - cannot upgrade Scanner Truth

6. Pattern against HTF bias:
   - confidence capped
   - must explain conflict

7. Pattern near opposing support/resistance:
   - quality capped
   - must add limited-room warning

8. Pattern target already reached:
   - status = exhausted
   - no fresh entry signal

Detailed rules by pattern:

Double top:
- Requires prior up-move
- Two swing highs within ATR-normalized tolerance
- Neckline between highs
- Forming until neckline breaks
- Confirmed only after neckline break with momentum or break/retest
- Invalidated if price closes above second high by threshold
- Must warn if target is into nearby support

Double bottom:
- Requires prior down-move
- Two swing lows within ATR-normalized tolerance
- Neckline between lows
- Forming until neckline breaks upward
- Confirmed only after neckline break with momentum or break/retest
- Invalidated if price closes below second low
- Must warn if target is into nearby resistance

Head and shoulders:
- Requires prior uptrend
- Left shoulder, head, right shoulder
- Head must be higher than both shoulders
- Shoulders within tolerance
- Neckline from troughs
- Forming until neckline break
- Confirmed only after neckline break/retest
- Invalidated if price reclaims right shoulder/head zone

Inverse head and shoulders:
- Requires prior downtrend
- Head lower than shoulders
- Neckline from peaks
- Confirmed only after neckline break/retest
- Invalidated if price breaks below right shoulder/head zone

Bull flag:
- Requires impulse leg up
- Pullback channel down or sideways
- Pullback retracement not too deep
- Confirmed on break of flag high
- Invalidated if price breaks impulse origin or deep retracement threshold
- Exhausted if measured target already hit

Bear flag:
- Requires impulse leg down
- Pullback channel up or sideways
- Confirmed on break of flag low
- Invalidated if price reclaims flag high
- Cap confidence if target is directly into major support

Ascending triangle:
- Flat resistance with rising lows
- Confirmed only on resistance break
- Failed if rising-low structure breaks
- Must distinguish accumulation from fake breakout

Descending triangle:
- Flat support with lower highs
- Confirmed only on support break
- Failed if lower-high structure breaks
- Cap if breakdown sells directly into HTF support

Symmetrical triangle:
- Lower highs and higher lows
- Compression required
- Direction neutral until breakout
- Confirmed only after break and close outside range
- No buy/sell bias before breakout unless HTF context strongly supports it

Breakout/retest:
- Break must clear level by threshold
- Retest must hold the broken level
- Confirmation requires rejection/continuation candle after retest
- Failed if price closes back inside range

Liquidity sweep:
- Price takes prior high/low
- Must close back inside range
- Confirmation requires reclaim/rejection candle
- No trade if only a wick exists without follow-through
- Must classify as trap risk until reclaim confirms

Rejection wick:
- Wick/body ratio must meet threshold
- Must occur at meaningful level
- Requires follow-through candle for confirmation
- Alone cannot create ready-now

Engulfing candle:
- Body must engulf prior candle body
- Stronger at support/resistance or after pullback
- Requires context; not valid mid-range alone

Market structure shift:
- Break of prior swing structure
- Must identify previous trend
- Confirmation requires follow-through or retest
- False-break risk if break is small/low momentum

Exhaustion move:
- Price extended by ATR distance from mean/VWAP/MA
- Momentum slowing or large wick
- Classify as too-late or reversal-watch, not chase
- Must prevent fresh continuation entry if target already played out

Run-on continuation / flare:
- Requires ignition candle
- Follow-through candles
- Pullback respect
- Spread/slippage acceptable
- Exhaustion state must override continuation state when extension is too high
