Validation must now grade:

1. Edge Quality

- profit factor
- average win/loss
- RR quality

2. Risk Survival
- max drawdown

- worst losing streak
- recovery time
- tail-risk exposure
- account survival probability

3. Statistical Reliability
- sample size
- confidence interval
- out-of-sample performance
- walk-forward stability
- Monte Carlo robustness
- overfitting risk

4. Market Regime Fit
- trend performance
- chop performance
- high-volatility performance
- low-liquidity performance
- news-risk performance
- session-specific performance

5. Execution Reality
- slippage sensitivity
- spread sensitivity
- latency sensitivity
- fill probability
- implementation shortfall
- broker reliability impact

6. Trader Behavior Impact
- performance after losses
- performance after overrides
- overtrading risk
- discipline impact
- cognitive-risk sensitivity

7. Edge Durability
- recent performance decay
- historical stability
- regime drift
- false approval trend
- false block trend
- confidence calibration drift

Rules:
- A strategy cannot pass validation based on profit alone.
- A strategy with strong profit but dangerous drawdown must fail or be restricted.
- A strategy with good backtest but poor out-of-sample results must fail.
- A strategy with good signals but poor execution quality must be restricted.
- A strategy that only works in one market regime must be labeled regime-specific.
- A strategy showing edge decay must be demoted or moved back to shadow mode.
- Monte Carlo stress testing must randomize trade order, slippage, spreads, and execution delay.
- Validation score must be explainable in plain English.
- Risk Governor can freeze validation promotion.
- Control Tower controls rollout authority.
- Black Box Vault stores every validation report.

Final rule:
Nothing earns live authority unless it proves it can survive bad markets, bad fills, trader behavior risk, and changing regimes.
