Rules:
- Evolution is sandbox-only.
- No evolved strategy can touch live money directly.
- No mutation can skip validation.
- Ecosystem health matters more than isolated strategy profit.
- Survival quality matters more than raw profit.
- Agents and strategies earn trust from measurable performance only.
- Weak strategies can be demoted, quarantined, retired, or archived.
- Strong strategies can gain testing priority, not automatic live authority.
- Evolution Constitution defines permanent safety laws.
- Forbidden mutations can never be promoted.
- Fraud detection must identify fake edge, overfitting, and fragile optimization.
- Ecosystem fitness must measure contribution to diversification, execution stability, behavioral stability, and systemic survival.
- Risk Governor and Control Tower outrank all AI Economy and Evolution decisions.
- All reputation changes, lifecycle transitions, mutations, sandbox tests, fraud flags, and ecosystem survival scores must be logged into the Black Box Vault.
- Keep all previous phase tests passing.
