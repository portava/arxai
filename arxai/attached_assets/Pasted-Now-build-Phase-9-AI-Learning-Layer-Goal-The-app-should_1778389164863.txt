Now build Phase 9: AI Learning Layer.

Goal:
The app should learn from completed trades and improve settings over time without pretending to guarantee profits.

Create folder:

/server/ai-learning

Create files:

1. tradeOutcomeAnalyzer.ts

Analyze every completed trade.

Input:
{
 symbol,
 marketType,
 strategy,
 confidence,
 entry,
 stopLoss,
 takeProfit,
 exit,
 profitLoss,
 session,
 marketCondition,
 newsRisk,
 volatilityState,
 technicalBias,
 macroBias,
 holdTimeMinutes
}

Return:
{
 outcome,
 mistakeTags,
 successTags,
 entryQuality,
 exitQuality,
 riskQuality,
 lesson,
 suggestedAdjustment
}

Mistake tags:
- entered during chop
- entered before news
- stop too tight
- stop too wide
- poor risk reward
- low confidence trade
- ignored trend
- late entry
- early exit
- overtrading
- revenge trade risk
- abnormal volatility

Success tags:
- trend aligned
- clean break of structure
- strong risk reward
- good session timing
- strong pullback entry
- volatility confirmed
- news avoided
- risk respected

2. strategyOptimizer.ts

Track performance by:
- symbol
- strategy
- session
- market condition
- confidence range
- day of week
- volatility state

Return:
{
 bestStrategies,
 worstStrategies,
 recommendedEnabledStrategies,
 recommendedDisabledStrategies,
 confidenceAdjustment,
 riskAdjustment,
 warning
}

Rules:
- Never increase risk aggressively.
- Only suggest risk reduction automatically.
- Do not auto-enable live trading.
- Do not overfit based on small sample size.
- Require at least 30 trades before giving strong recommendations.

3. aiCoach.ts

Create an AI-style explanation engine.

For each completed trade, write:
- What happened?
- Was the setup valid?
- What could have been better?
- Should this strategy be adjusted?
- Should this market be avoided under similar conditions?

4. learningDashboard.tsx

Frontend page:
AI Learning Center

Show:
- top performing symbols
- worst performing symbols
- top performing strategies
- worst performing strategies
- common mistake tags
- best trading sessions
- worst trading sessions
- AI lessons
- recommended changes
- sample size warning

Add button:
“Apply conservative improvements”

This can only:
- raise minimum confidence
- lower max trades
- lower risk per trade
- disable poorly performing strategy
- increase cooldown

It cannot:
- increase risk
- enable live trading
- remove kill switch
- lower confidence automatically
