Goal:
Create an automatic weekly review system that summarizes trader performance, identifies patterns, and gives the user a focused improvement plan for the next week.

Find:
- trade journal entries
- trader scores

- AI coach notes

- market intelligence reports
- live trade history

3. Build Weekly Review Service

The service should calculate:

- weekly P/L
- win rate
- average R:R
- best/worst trade
- best/worst strategy
- best/worst trading session
- most common mistake tag
- most common strength tag
- discipline score trend
- execution score trend
- emotional control trend
- consistency trend
- replay practice trend
- journal behavior trend

4. Generate AI Weekly Summary

The AI should explain:

- what improved
- what got worse
- what caused most losses
- what created most wins
- what behavior repeated
- what market conditions helped or hurt
- what strategy should be used more
- what strategy should be reduced
- one main focus for next week

Tone:
- professional
- direct
- calm
- no hype
- no guaranteed profits

5. Add weekly goals

Create 1–3 improvement goals each week.

Examples:
- “Wait for confirmation before entering.”
- “Limit trades to 3 per day.”
- “Only take setups with 2:1 R:R or better.”
- “Avoid trading after 2 consecutive losses.”
- “Complete 3 replay drills before live trading.”
