PART 2 — CORE ARCHITECTURE

Create/upgrade the ARX Fund Book architecture:

1. Broker Mirror Layer
    Mirrors real broker account state exactly as received from the bridge.
2. Master Fund Book
    Tracks total internal fund accounting across all investors and pools.
3. Strategy Pool Ledger
    Tracks Conservative, Balanced, Aggressive, and Cash Reserve pools.
4. Unit-Based NAV Engine
    Calculates pool NAV and investor unit value.
5. Investor Ledger
    Tracks each investor’s deposits, withdrawals, fees, P/L, adjustments, locked funds, withdrawable funds, and current value.
6. Investor Pool Holdings
    Tracks how many units each investor owns in each pool.
7. Trade-to-Pool Allocation
    Maps live broker positions/trades to strategy pools.
8. Real-Time P/L Allocator
    Applies pool P/L to investor values based on units.
9. Drawdown Engine
    Tracks master, pool, investor, broker, and trade drawdown.
10. Discrepancy/Reconciliation Center
    Compares broker mirror, pool NAV, investor ledger, and displayed values.
11. Capital Movement System
    Handles deposit requests, withdrawal requests, speed-based fees, 30-day deposit lock, settlement status, and admin approvals.
12. Weekly Investor Explanation Engine
    Creates weekly dashboard explanations showing why the investor gained or lost money.
13. Admin Internal Profit Waterfall
    Calculates ARX internal 60% share and investor 40% distributable share, with ARX details visible only to admin.
14. Audit + Statement System
    Tracks every financial change and produces transparent investor records.

PART 6 — UNIT-BASED NAV ACCOUNTING

Implement unit-based NAV accounting.

Core rule:
Units are not fixed-limited supply.
Units are variable-supply internal accounting shares.

Units are only created when:

* deposit is approved/settled
* allocation is approved
* NAV is confirmed
* audit log is created

Units are redeemed when:

* withdrawal is approved
* pool redemption is processed
* NAV is confirmed
* audit log is created

Pool NAV formula:
Pool NAV = Pool Net Value / Total Pool Units

Pool Net Value =
Starting Pool Capital

* Realized P/L
* Unrealized P/L
* Deposits allocated to the pool

* Withdrawals redeemed from the pool
* Fees
    +/- Approved Adjustments

Investor Pool Value =
Investor Pool Units × Current Pool NAV

Investor Ownership % =
Investor Pool Units / Total Pool Units

Investor Gain Share =
Pool Gain × Investor Ownership %

Investor Unrealized P/L =
Pool Open P/L × Investor Ownership %

Investor Total Value =
Cash Reserve

* Conservative Units × Conservative NAV
* Balanced Units × Balanced NAV
* Aggressive Units × Aggressive NAV

* Fees charged to investor
    +/- Approved Adjustments

Important:
Deposits increase pool value and total units.
Deposits must not artificially increase NAV.

Withdrawals reduce pool value and redeem units.
Withdrawals must not artificially decrease NAV.

Trading performance changes NAV.
Fees and approved adjustments affect net investor value.

====================================================
PART 7 — STARTING NAV AND UNIT ISSUANCE

Each strategy pool starts at:

Starting NAV = $1.00 per unit

New Units Issued =
Net Deposit Allocated to Pool / Current Pool NAV

Units Redeemed =
Gross Withdrawal From Pool / Current Pool NAV

Investor Pool Value =
Investor Units × Current Pool NAV

Do not create arbitrary units.
Do not issue units without approved ledger entry.
Do not redeem units without approved withdrawal/capital movement entry.
Every unit issuance/redemption must be auditable.

PART 8 — 60/40 PROFIT WATERFALL

Implement internal 60/40 profit waterfall.

Rule:
When a strategy pool produces eligible net new profit, ARX receives 60% for operations, trader management, technology, risk reserve, compliance/legal, infrastructure, and business spending. Investors receive 40% as the investor distributable profit.

Investor 40% is distributed by NAV/unit ownership.

Formula:

Eligible Net New Profit =
max(0, Verified Pool Value - Pool High-Water Mark - Net New Deposits)

ARX Share =
Eligible Net New Profit × 60%

Investor Distributable Profit =
Eligible Net New Profit × 40%

Investor Profit Share =
Investor Distributable Profit × (Investor Units / Total Pool Units)

Important:
Do not apply 60/40 split to losses.
Losses reduce pool NAV and investor value by units.

Do not pay ARX 60% while pool/investor is still below high-water mark.
ARX 60% only applies to eligible net new profit above high-water mark.

If pool is below high-water mark:

* ARX Share = $0
* Investor value recovers through NAV movement
* no ARX performance allocation applies until recovery above high-water mark

Investor-facing:
Investors should not see ARX internal share, trader compensation, or reserve breakdown.

Investor-facing should show only:

* their current value
* their gain/loss
* their investor distributable gain/loss
* allocation amount by strategy
* locked amount
* withdrawable amount
* fees charged to them
* weekly explanation of why they gained or lost

Investor-facing wording example:
“Your account gained $400 this week based on your active pool units and the investor distributable portion of verified weekly performance.”

Do not show:

* “ARX made $6,000”
* “Trader compensation was $1,200”
* “Company reserve received $800”
* “ARX internal split”

Admin-only:
Show full waterfall:

* gross pool profit
* eligible net new profit
* ARX 60% share
* investor 40% distributable
* investor-by-investor distribution
* company operations share
* trader compensation share
* technology/data reserve
* legal/compliance reserve
* risk reserve
* approval status
* reconciliation status

PART 9 — INTERNAL ARX SPLIT

ARX 60% share should be internally split in admin-only view.

Make this configurable by admin.

Default internal split example:

* Company operations: 40% of ARX share
* Trader compensation pool: 35% of ARX share
* Technology/AI/data/infrastructure: 15% of ARX share
* Risk/legal/compliance reserve: 10% of ARX share

Trader compensation should be risk-adjusted.

Trader Compensation =
Trader Compensation Pool × Trader Performance Weight × Risk Quality Multiplier

Risk Quality Multiplier:

* 1.00 = profitable, clean risk, within drawdown limits
* 0.75 = profitable, but elevated drawdown
* 0.50 = profitable, but risk warnings occurred
* 0.00 = loss period, below high-water mark, or rule violation

No trader performance bonus should be paid when:

* pool is below high-water mark
* investor distributable profit is $0
* drawdown limits were breached without approval
* trader violated risk rules
* trades were not assigned/reconciled

This entire section is admin-only.
Investors must not see trader payroll or ARX internal allocation.

PART 13 — 30-DAY DEPOSIT LOCK RULE

Add mandatory 30-day lock for every new deposit.

Rule:
Every deposit is locked for 30 calendar days from the date the deposit is marked Settled/Completed and units are issued.

During the lock:

* investor can see the funds
* investor can see performance
* investor can gain or lose based on pool allocation
* investor can see the unlock date
* investor cannot withdraw those locked funds
* investor cannot include those locked units in available withdrawal amount

Investor-facing wording:
“This deposit is locked for 30 days to protect pool stability. Unlock date: ___.”

Do not hide the money.

Show it clearly as:

* Current Value
* Locked Value
* Unlock Date
* Days Remaining

Lock starts when funds are actually settled/approved and units are issued, not merely when the investor clicks submit.

Formula:
lockEndDate = unitsIssuedAt + 30 calendar days

Available to Withdraw must exclude locked value.

Investor available balance formula:
Available to Withdraw =
Unlocked Cash Reserve

* Unlocked Pool Units Redeemable Value

* Pending Withdrawals
* Reserved Fees
* Any active freeze/hold

Locked funds must still be included in:

* Current Value
* Allocation
* P/L
* Drawdown
* Performance reports
* Weekly reports

Locked funds must not be included in:

* Available to Withdraw
* Rush withdrawal availability
* Full withdrawal availability unless admin override is legally/policy allowed

Default:
30-day lock cannot be bypassed by investor.

Optional admin override:
Only admin can override if policy allows.
Override requires:

* admin reason
* audit log
* compliance flag
* investor acknowledgment if possible
