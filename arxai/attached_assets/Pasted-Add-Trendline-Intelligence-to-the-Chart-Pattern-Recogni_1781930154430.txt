Trendline rules:

Uptrend support trendline:
- requires at least 2 meaningful swing lows
- 3 touches improves quality
- higher lows preferred
- respected if price reacts from line
- broken only if candle closes below line by ATR-normalized threshold
- confirmed break requires follow-through or failed retest
- invalidated break if price reclaims line quickly

Downtrend resistance trendline:
- requires at least 2 meaningful swing highs
- 3 touches improves quality
- lower highs preferred
- respected if price rejects line
- broken only if candle closes above line by ATR-normalized threshold
- confirmed break requires follow-through or retest hold
- invalidated break if price falls back below line quickly

Parallel channel:
- requires support and resistance trendlines with similar slope
- upper/lower touches
- channel break must say which side broke
- failed channel break returns to range/channel status

Trendline break:
- do not count wick-only pierces as confirmed breaks
- require close beyond line
- require ATR-normalized break distance
- require follow-through or retest confirmation for high confidence
- if break is low momentum, label as weak/possible fakeout

Trendline retest:
- after break, price returns to line
- support-to-resistance or resistance-to-support behavior is detected
- rejection/hold confirms the retest
- failed retest cancels or weakens the break thesis

Trendline change / trend shift:
Detect when:
- uptrend support breaks and starts forming lower highs
- downtrend resistance breaks and starts forming higher lows
- trendline slope flattens into range
- range breaks into directional trend
- previous pattern invalidates and a new pattern begins

Pattern change:
Eleanor should detect pattern transitions like:
- triangle forming → breakout confirmed
- bull flag forming → failed flag / range
- breakout → failed breakout / trap
- uptrend channel → broken trend / reversal watch
- downtrend channel → breakout / reversal watch
- range → trend continuation
- continuation pattern → exhaustion / too late

Examples:

Example 1:
Decision: WAIT
Why: Price broke the uptrend support trendline, but the break is not confirmed yet.
Evidence:
- Structure: Higher lows are weakening.
- Trendline: Uptrend support broke on close, but no retest has confirmed.
- Pattern: Prior bull flag is transitioning into range/reversal watch.
Confirmation: Bearish shift confirms if price retests the broken trendline and rejects.
Invalidation: Bullish thesis returns if price reclaims the trendline and holds above it.
Trader Test: Draw the prior swing-low trendline and watch the retest.
Risk Note: Do not chase the first break without confirmation.

Example 2:
Decision: Conditional BUY
Why: Price broke above the downtrend resistance trendline and is retesting it as support.
Evidence:
- Structure: Lower-high sequence is weakening.
- Trendline: Downtrend line broke and is being retested.
- Pattern: Possible reversal or breakout-retest.
Confirmation: Buy strengthens if the retest holds and price makes a higher low.
Invalidation: Cancel if price closes back under the broken trendline.
Trader Test: Check whether the retest candle holds above the old resistance line.
Risk Note: Failed retests often trap breakout buyers.

Tests:
1. Uptrend support trendline detected from rising swing lows.
2. Downtrend resistance trendline detected from falling swing highs.
3. Third touch increases trendline quality.
4. Wick-only break does not confirm trendline break.

8. Downtrend line break + retest hold confirms resistance-to-support flip.
9. Parallel channel detected from matching slopes.
