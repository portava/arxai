PART 3 — Risk Governor Integration

Risk Governor must check the Account Rules Engine before queueing any:
- open trade
- partial close
- full close
- move stop
- modify TP/SL
- cancel order

Rules:
- Opening new trades can be blocked by account rules.
- Risk-reducing actions should remain allowed unless emergency stop blocks all.
- If close-only is triggered, new trades are blocked but closing/reducing risk is allowed.
- If stop loss is required, trade ticket must require SL before confirmation.
- If news trading is blocked, new trades during blackout are blocked.
- If max daily loss is near breach, warn or block based on profile settings.
