BUILD PHASE — Operator-Funded 10-User Live Pilot

Use the current codebase. Do not rebuild unrelated systems.

Goal:
Allow exactly 10 approved private beta users to trade live using operator-owned capital only. Users are not depositing money, withdrawing money, investing money, or receiving ownership of the master account. Each user receives a controlled operator-assigned trading allocation for testing under strict risk limits.

Important:
Do not create public live trading.
Do not describe this as deposits, withdrawals, custody, investor funds, or user-owned broker balance.
Do not enable profit-sharing, payouts, or user withdrawals in this phase.
Do not bypass legal/compliance flags.
Do not bypass admin approval.
Do not bypass disclosures.
Do not bypass risk limits.
Do not bypass bridge health, shared master mapping, queue, exposure, cooldown, kill switch, close-only, force-review, or confirmation gates.
Do not create live commands during build or QA.
