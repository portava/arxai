Polish ARX Market Scanner Layout, Opportunity Map, Chart Read, Truth Labels, and Action States

Goal:
Clean up the ARX Market Scanner screen layout shown in the screenshots. The scanner is functional, but the UI is crowded, vertically clipped, and visually inconsistent between the scanner header, Opportunity Map, chart area, Eleanor Chart Read, Trade Health, and action cards.

This is a UI/UX formatting pass only.

Do not change:
- scanner scoring
- edge score logic
- market feed/data source
- candle calculations
- chart overlays
- Eleanor reasoning logic
- scanner decision logic
- trade execution gates
- risk gates
- one-click behavior
- MT5 bridge logic
- order placement
- live-trade behavior
- position management
- backend market analysis truth

Only change:
- layout
- spacing
- alignment
- wrapping
- card composition
- visual hierarchy
- responsive behavior
- loading/empty/error presentation
- stale/freshness labels
- snapshot/truth labels
- mismatch warnings
- action-state wording

Core rule:
Make the scanner cleaner, faster to read, and more honest without making conditional, stale, weak, or waiting setups look more trade-ready than they really are.

- what files will be changed

PHASE 33 — Final Acceptance Criteria

The scanner polish is complete when:

- Top content is no longer clipped.
- Header, decision strip, chart, Opportunity Map, Eleanor, and Trade Health align visually.
- A trader can see symbol, decision, readiness, confidence, and freshness immediately.
- All modules show freshness/source/snapshot context where available.
- Conditional/WAIT states are impossible to mistake for confirmed trades.
- Stale/mismatched data is clearly labeled.
- Opportunity Map groups Ready / Waiting / Developing / Stale setups.
- Opportunity cards clearly show readiness and blockers.
- Selecting an opportunity updates chart/Eleanor/context consistently or clearly opens details.
- Snapshot details are inspectable.
- Rescan feedback is clear.
- Rescan/loading/error states are clean.
- Trade Health uses real bridge/position truth.
- Floating Eleanor button does not cover important content.
- Scroll position does not jump annoyingly.
- UI is screenshot-clean without hiding risk truth.
- Keyboard/accessibility basics work.
- Responsive layout works.
- Tests pass.
- No scanner/trading/backend/Eleanor/feed/risk/bridge/execution logic changed.

Final lock:
Do not make conditional, stale, weak, or waiting setups look more trade-ready than they are. The UI should improve readability and trust, not inflate confidence.
