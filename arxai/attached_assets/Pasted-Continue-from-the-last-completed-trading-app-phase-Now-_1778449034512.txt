PHASE 3 — Build Strategy Analysis Service

Create a service that analyzes:

- Which strategies perform best
- Which strategies perform worst
- Best symbols per strategy
- Best timeframes per strategy
- Best sessions per strategy
- Best market conditions per strategy
- Worst market conditions per strategy
- Average risk-to-reward per strategy
- Expectancy per strategy
- Emotional discipline per strategy
- Execution quality per strategy

The system should detect patterns like:

- “This trader performs best with breakout setups during strong trend conditions.”
- “This trader loses most during choppy markets.”
- “This strategy performs poorly after two losing trades.”
- “This trader should avoid scalping during low-liquidity sessions.”

PHASE 4 — Add Strategy Fit Score

Create a Strategy Fit Score from 0–100.

- Historical win rate
- Average R:R
- Expectancy
- Discipline score
- Emotional control
- Market condition match
- Session match
- Symbol match
- Timeframe match

Status: Strong Match
