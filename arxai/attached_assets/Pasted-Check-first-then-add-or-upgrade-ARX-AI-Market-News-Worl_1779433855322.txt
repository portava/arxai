Check first, then add or upgrade ARX AI Market News + World Event Intelligence.

SCOPE LOCK:
Only work on:
1. News/event data services.
2. Economic calendar/news feed integrations.
3. Public social/post monitoring where provider access already exists or can be safely configured.
4. Scanner news-risk integration.
5. Ruby/AI news intelligence context.
6. Trade ticket news-risk precheck.
7. Admin/source configuration and clean status display if needed.

Do not:
- Enable live trading.
- Place trades.
- Change MT5 execution permissions.
- Change kill switches.
- Change admin approval rules.
- Change trade execution logic.
- Change login/auth.
- Redesign unrelated pages.
- Scrape platforms illegally or bypass API limits.
- Show fake news as real.

GOAL:
ARX AI scanner and Ruby should constantly monitor market-moving news, economic events, world leader statements, central bank decisions, geopolitical developments, and global economic conditions that could affect trades.

The AI should use this information when:
- ranking scanner results
- explaining a market
- deciding whether a setup is safe to watch
- warning users before opening BUY/SELL trade tickets
- answering trading questions
- identifying when news risk makes a trade dangerous
- spotting markets worth watching because of breaking catalysts

PHASE 2 — MARKET NEWS INTELLIGENCE SERVICE
Create or upgrade a normalized MarketNewsIntelligenceService.

It should ingest/normalize:
- economic calendar events
- CPI / inflation data
- jobs reports
- unemployment data
- interest-rate decisions
- central bank speeches
- Fed / ECB / BOJ / BOE decisions
- GDP data
- retail sales
- PMI
- bond yields if provider supports it
- oil/energy news
- commodity news
- crypto regulatory news
- stock market earnings/news if relevant
- geopolitical conflict/escalation news
- trade tariffs/sanctions
- world leader statements
- government policy decisions
- major public posts from configured official accounts or monitored leaders

Social/public post monitoring:
- Use official APIs only if configured, such as X API.
- Do not bypass platform rules.
- Allow a configurable watchlist of official/public accounts, such as major world leaders, central banks, treasury/finance ministries, economic agencies, and high-impact figures.
- Include Donald Trump/public world leader posts only through lawful/available provider feeds or official API access.
- If social feed access is not configured, show “social feed not configured” cleanly and rely on news providers that quote/report those posts.

Do not expose raw provider payloads to users.

PHASE 4 — NEWS RISK SCORING
Create or upgrade a NewsRiskEngine.

For each symbol/market, calculate:
- current news risk: none / low / medium / high / critical
- directional news bias: bullish / bearish / mixed / unclear
- affected currency/asset side
- time sensitivity
- source confidence
- volatility risk
- event proximity risk
- conflict between technical setup and news
- whether to watch, avoid, or wait

Examples:
- EURUSD: USD bullish if Fed surprise hawkish.
- XAUUSD: risk high if inflation/Fed/yield news active.
- BTCUSD: risk high if regulatory or ETF/liquidity news hits.
- US indices: risk high around CPI/FOMC/jobs/earnings shocks.
- Oil-related assets: risk high around OPEC/geopolitical conflict.

Upgrade Market Scanner so news intelligence affects market ranking.

- News Risk

- Economic Event Nearby
- World Leader / Policy Risk
- Central Bank Risk
- Geopolitical Risk
- News Bias
- Watch / Avoid / Wait recommendation

Scanner ranking should consider:
- current technical setup
- historical/backtest results
- volatility
- spread/market conditions
- economic calendar
- fresh news catalysts
- sentiment/news bias
- risk of sudden reversal due to news

If a setup looks good technically but news risk is high, scanner should clearly warn:
“Technical setup is strong, but news risk is high. Wait for confirmation.”

PHASE 6 — RUBY / AI INTEGRATION
Ruby must use news intelligence in all trading-related answers.

When user asks:
- “What should I trade?”
- “Should I buy or sell?”
- “Analyze EURUSD”
- “Why is gold moving?”
- “Is this a fakeout?”
- “Should I close?”
- “What markets should I watch?”
- “Check scanner”
- “What’s risky right now?”

Ruby should receive a structured MarketNewsDecisionPack with:
- latest relevant news
- economic calendar events
- world leader/public policy statements if available
- central bank events
- geopolitical risk
- affected symbols/assets
- news sentiment/bias
- event freshness
- confidence/source quality
- historical/backtest context
- current technical context
- unavailable data warnings

Ruby should explain clearly:
- what news matters
- why it matters
- which asset/currency it affects
- whether it supports or conflicts with the technical setup
- whether to watch, wait, avoid, or proceed carefully

Ruby must not hallucinate news.
If news feed is unavailable:
“News feed is unavailable right now, so I’m relying on chart/history/scanner data only.”
