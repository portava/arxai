==================================================

Create scoring model:

Opportunity Score = 0 to 100

Factors:

- trend alignment: 0-15
- support/resistance quality: 0-15
- entry timing: 0-15
- risk/reward quality: 0-15
- volatility condition: 0-10
- spread condition: 0-10
- strategy match: 0-10
- AI confidence calibration: 0-10

- 90-100: ELITE
- 80-89: STRONG

Add this score to:
