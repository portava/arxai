SECTION 2 — Shooting Star detector

Add a dedicated ShootingStarTruth detector.

Definition:
A shooting star is a bearish reversal candle that appears after an uptrend or bullish move. It has:
- small real body near the candle low
- long upper wick
- little or no lower wick
- price pushed higher intrabar but closed near the low
- strongest near resistance, pivot, upper channel, or after extension

Required measurements:
- bodySize
- upperWickSize
- lowerWickSize
- candleRange
- upperWickToBodyRatio
- bodyPositionPercent
- priorTrendStrength
- distanceFromSupportResistance
- ATR extension
- volume/tick-volume spike if available
- next candle confirmation

ShootingStarTruthVerdict:
- detected: boolean
- status: forming / unconfirmed / confirmed / failed
- locationQuality: poor / fair / good / strong
- priorTrend: uptrend / range / downtrend / unknown
- upperWickRatio
- bodyNearLow: boolean
- nearResistance: boolean
- nearPivotResistance: boolean
- exhaustionContext: boolean
- confirmationLevel
- invalidationLevel
- bearishBias: none / weak / moderate / strong
- warnings[]

Rules:
- Do not classify shooting star as valid bearish reversal without prior uptrend or bullish push.
- Do not confirm from candle shape alone.
- Confirm only if next candle closes below shooting star low or price breaks supporting structure.
- Invalidate if price breaks above shooting star high and holds.
- Strongest if at resistance, R1/R2/R3, upper trendline, prior high, or after ATR extension.
- Weak if mid-range.
- Weak if feed is stale/historical.
- Weak if lower wick is large.
- Weak if candle body is not near the low.
- Weak if there is no room to target.
- Mark too-late if price already dumped far from the trigger before entry.

Eleanor example:
Decision: WAIT / Conditional SELL
Why: A shooting star formed after an upward push, but confirmation is still needed.
Evidence:
- Candlestick: Shooting star unconfirmed.
- Location: Near resistance/R1.
- Trend: Prior bullish push is extended.
- Timing: Wait for next candle close below the shooting star low.
Confirmation: Sell thesis strengthens below [level].
Invalidation: Cancel if price closes above the shooting star high.
Risk Note: Do not short the wick alone without confirmation.

SECTION 4 — Reversal logic

A reversal is not valid just because a candle appears.

Reversal requires at least one of:
- prior trend exhaustion
- failure at major level
- break of structure
- trendline break
- lower high after bullish trend
- higher low after bearish trend
- liquidity sweep and reclaim
- candle confirmation
- volume/order-flow support if available
- pivot rejection
- support/resistance flip

Reversal statuses:
- early warning
- forming
- conditional
- confirmed
- failed
- too_late

Rules:
- Early reversal warning cannot create ready status.
- Confirmed reversal still needs entry, timing, risk, and feed approval.
- Reversal against strong HTF trend should be downgraded.
- Reversal into nearby support/resistance should be downgraded.
- Reversal after full target move should be too_late.

SECTION 5 — Continuation logic

A continuation pattern is not valid until the trend resumes.

Continuation requires:
- existing trend
- pause/consolidation
- controlled pullback or compression
- breakout/retest or continuation trigger
- target room
- no major contradiction from higher timeframe
- timing approval

Continuation statuses:
- forming
- waiting_breakout
- waiting_retest
- confirmed
- failed
- exhausted

Rules:
- Triangle/flag/pennant forming = wait.
- Breakout wick only = unconfirmed.
- Clean close + retest = stronger.
- Failed breakout = trap/failure.
- Continuation into major opposing level = downgraded.
- Continuation after extended move = exhaustion warning.

SECTION 6 — Consolidation logic

Consolidation should usually reduce trade aggression.

Consolidation detector should identify:
- range high
- range low
- midpoint
- compression
- candle overlap
- lower ATR
- inside-bar cluster
- repeated failed breaks
- balanced pressure

Consolidation statuses:
- range
- compression
- breakout_pending
- false_break
- confirmed_breakout
- no_edge

Rules:
- Mid-range = no edge.
- Range edge = possible bounce setup only with confirmation.
- Breakout requires close outside range.
- Failed breakout returns to range or trap.
- Compression requires wait for expansion.
- No directional bias while price is trapped inside range.

Classification rules:

- Sell candidate requires bearish direction + valid location + entry confirmation.
- Scalp candidate requires short-term timing + tight invalidation + spread/liquidity approval.
- Consolidation no-trade if price is mid-range or choppy.
- Too late if entry trigger is far behind current price.
- Blocked if feed/trade health/risk fails.
