- userId
- type
- title
- message
- severity: INFO, WARNING, CRITICAL
- source: AI, SYSTEM, MARKET_DATA, BRIDGE, TRADE, RISK, SCANNER

- read/unread

- createdAt
- readAt
- metadata

- claim certainty
- invent market data or TP/SL levels
AI close warnings must say:
“AI alert only — review manually. No trade was closed.”
