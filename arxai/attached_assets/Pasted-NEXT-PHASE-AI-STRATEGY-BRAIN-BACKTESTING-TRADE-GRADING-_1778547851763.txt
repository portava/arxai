2. CREATE AI MARKET ANALYST
==================================================

Create an AI Market Analyst module that can review a symbol and produce a structured analysis.

It should analyze:

- trend direction
- market structure
- support/resistance zones
- volatility
- spread condition
- recent candle behavior
- higher-timeframe bias
- lower-timeframe entry condition
- risk/reward quality
- setup quality
- invalidation level
- whether to trade now or wait

Output format:

{
  "symbol": "XAUUSD",
  "timeframe": "M15",
  "marketBias": "bullish | bearish | neutral | choppy",
  "trendStrength": 0-100,
  "setupQualityScore": 0-100,
  "entryQualityScore": 0-100,
  "riskScore": 0-100,
  "confidenceScore": 0-100,
  "recommendedAction": "BUY | SELL | WAIT | REJECT",
  "entryZone": {},
  "stopLoss": number,
  "takeProfit": number,
  "riskRewardRatio": number,
  "reasonForTrade": "...",
  "reasonToAvoid": "...",
  "invalidationReason": "...",
  "rulesPassed": [],
  "rulesFailed": []
}

Important:
If the data source is simulator/tester data, clearly mark:

dataSource = SIMULATOR

Do not pretend it is broker data.

4. CREATE MARKET REPLAY MODE
==================================================

Create page:

/market-replay

Market replay should allow the user to replay candles step by step.

Features:

- choose symbol
- choose timeframe
- choose strategy
- play/pause
- step forward one candle
- speed control
- AI decision at each candle
- show why AI would enter, wait, or reject
- allow tester to compare human decision vs AI decision

Store every replay decision as:

- replayId
- candleIndex
- symbol
- timeframe
- aiAction
- humanAction optional
- entry
- stopLoss
- takeProfit
- confidenceScore
- riskScore
- reason
- outcome

Every trade or live-intent should receive a grade.

Grades:

Score trade on:

- trend alignment
- entry timing

- risk/reward
- position size
- confidence quality
- spread condition
- rule compliance
- emotional discipline
- overtrade risk

Output:

  "strengths": [],
  "weaknesses": [],
  "mistakesDetected": [],
  "improvementSuggestion": "...",
  "shouldHaveTakenTrade": true/false
}
