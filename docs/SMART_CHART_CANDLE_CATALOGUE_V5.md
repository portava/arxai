# Smart Chart candle catalogue v5: three-to-five-candle reversal shapes

`arx-candle-geometry-v5` adds 7 named observations to the v4 checkpoint. There are now 78 catalogue definitions in 19 shared geometry families; the new names join existing families, so the family count is unchanged. With three preserved initial-core names, 81 of the owner's 104 entries have implementations and focused fixtures; the other 23 remain in `SMART_CHART_CANDLE_COVERAGE.json`. These are named coverage counts, not independent signals, predictive reliability, application completion or parity with another chart product. No competitive scorecard row changes status.

All new findings remain **observed**, with the ending candle's actual close time, and keep every v4 evidence, finality, interval, causality and cap rule. Ladder Bottom reuses v4's 5-candle window (`contiguous(closed,i-4,i)`), which never reads before index 0 or across a missing interval. Prior trends use the existing five-close guard and exclude the formation. No confidence percentage, prediction, execution instruction or confirmation status is added.

## Definitions and differences

The conventions are the same as in v4. Means use the prior ten closed bars excluding the evaluated candle. "Long" is a body above that mean. "Short" is a positive body below half of it. The equal analogue is 0.05 × the prior-10 mean range. Colour is strict, and every name requires a measured ARX prior trend. Each tolerance is a null-checked value; no comparison runs without its baseline.

| Name and family | ARX criteria | TA-Lib conditions as read | ARX-vs-TA-Lib differences |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Identical Three Crows (`soldiers_crows`) | Three down candles, falling closes; the 2nd open within 0.05 × the prior-10 mean range before the 1st candle of the 1st close, the 3rd open within 0.05 × the range before the 2nd candle of the 2nd close; each lower shadow below 0.1 × its own prior-10 mean range. No body rule. | Three black, declining closes; each lower shadow < ShadowVeryShort at its index; opens within Equal of the prior close. | Prior trend; Equal analogue on prior-10 instead of 5-bar. An open inside the prior body by more than the tolerance stays Three Black Crows only; within the tolerance, and strictly inside the body, both names can apply and share one summary slot. |
| Two Crows (`soldiers_crows`) | Long up 1st; down 2nd with its body strictly gapped above; down 3rd opening strictly inside the 2nd body and closing strictly inside the 1st body. | Same conditions. | Prior trend and strict colour only. |
| Upside Gap Two Crows (`soldiers_crows`) | Long up 1st; short down 2nd with a body gap; down 3rd opening strictly above the 2nd open, closing strictly below the 2nd close and strictly above the 1st close. | 2nd ≤ BodyShort; same gap, engulfment and close rules. | Prior trend; ARX Short (< half the mean body) is stricter than TA-Lib's ≤ mean body. ### Opposite labels on the same bar

The core Engulfing detector runs separately. **Stick Sandwich** and the core Bearish Engulfing can both fire on the final two candles, with opposite direction labels. This happens when the third open is at or above the middle close, the third body is larger than the middle body, and the middle close is above the close four bars before the final candle (the core four-bar prior-rise rule). The default fixture does not meet this. A tested variant does, and it records the summary as the core Bearish Engulfing, a Closing Marubozu, then Stick Sandwich, as separate findings in separate families. **Unique Three-River Bottom** could meet the core Bullish Engulfing in theory, but only when the second body is smaller than the short third body and the other core conditions hold. The fixture asserts that it does not occur there; no wider case is tested. As in v4, same-bar ties are ordered by status, scale and then id, and the global ranking is unchanged.

Each new name has the per-fixture positive, flat, scale, incomplete-candle and future-prefix checks, plus the trend-context control. Near misses cover Identical Three Crows open tolerance and lower shadow, Two Crows close and open placement, Upside Gap Two Crows engulfment and gap forms, Stick Sandwich close tolerance and middle colour, Unique Three-River short third body and lower second low, Three Stars range containment and second shadow, and Ladder Bottom shadow, opens and window gaps. **Review follow-up (2026-10-28, same `arx-candle-geometry-v5` rules, no detector change).** A mutation review found published criteria in both v4 and v5 whose deletion or loosening no test caught. The suite then gained a one-predicate near miss, each next to a passing control, for each criterion that review listed, including the tolerance boundaries (Side-by-Side opens .12/.13 and lengths .63/.64 against .127/.635; Identical Three Crows third open and both later lower shadows; Stick Sandwich .12/.13 and the reference-index control at .1248; Ladder Bottom upper shadow .245 against .247), the Three Stars in the South predicates that review named, the first-body long rules and the zero-body short rule. Two window tests were added for all 4- and 5-candle ids: a gap right after the first formation candle is refused, and a prefix that shows no trend is not turned into one by formation bars. Each of that review's 106 recorded mutants fails the suite. That did not make every published criterion pinned; the correction below lists what a later review still found deletable. The plain-language meaning for Three Stars in the South no longer says the bodies and ranges shrink progressively, which the rule does not check; the scanner's explanation no longer says "narrowed". The Rising/Falling Three Methods meanings now say that the reaction *bodies* stayed within the first high-low range, since shadows are not checked.

**Correction and mechanical mutation pass (2026-10-28, same `arx-candle-geometry-v5` rules, no detector change).** An earlier version of the paragraph above said the suite pinned "every such criterion" and "every Three Stars in the South predicate". Neither was true. A later review showed, with the suite passing, that these could still be deleted: the Three Stars colours of the second and third candles and the third-high bound; the colours of the second and third Three Methods reactions and the first reaction's containment; the Mat Hold rule that the fifth close clears the *highest* reaction high; the white third Side-by-Side line; the black first Stick Sandwich candle; and four Ladder Bottom colours.

A script then deleted every `&&` operand and every `||` operand in the v4/v5 detection code, one at a time. The code covered is the `isShortAt` helper and the scanner from Gap Three Methods through Mat Hold. That gives 210 mutants (193 conjuncts, 17 disjuncts). With the tests below, 190 of them fail `test:candlestick-catalogue`; a rerun after the round-4 tests gives the same 190. The other 20 pass. An earlier version of this paragraph said all 20 were equivalent, meaning that no input the scanner accepts as valid closed evidence tells them apart from the shipped rule. That is shown below for 18 of them. It is false for the Three-Line Strike `n2` null check, which a subnormal-price input tells apart. For the Three Stars `nearRange` null check it holds at subnormal prices, but overflowing ranges were not analysed.

- **`isShortAt` mean null check.** A missing mean makes the threshold 0, and a positive body is never below 0.
- **The six Gap Three Methods colour checks.** Each colour already follows from the open and close placement. For example, on the upside the third open lies inside the second body and the third close lies inside the first body, so the third candle must be down.
- **The three Identical Three Crows null checks (`e1`, `nearRange`, `meanRange`) and the Three Stars `meanRange` null check.** With a missing baseline, the strict shadow test becomes "shadow < 0", which never holds.
- **Two Crows and Upside Gap Two Crows third colour.** It follows from the third open and close placement once the middle candle is down.
- **Unique Three-River first colour.** A black second candle that opens at or below the first open and closes above the first close forces a black first candle. This holds to within the 1e-12 relative tolerance.
- **Three-Line Strike strike colour, on both sides.** Progressing closes, an open beyond the third close and a close beyond the first open force the strike colour.
- **`i>=3` and `i>=4`.** `contiguous()` already refuses a negative start.
- **The Three-Line Strike `n2` null check: not equivalent.** For ordinary prices the `n2` window (the ten bars before the second candle) contains the first candle, whose range is positive, so `n2` exists whenever `n1` does. `priorMean` divides each range by 10 before it sums, and at subnormal prices that division can round every term to 0, so `n2` can be missing while `n1` exists. A constructed input shows this. It has 15 contiguous closed 300-second bars; every price is an integer multiple u of `Number.MIN_VALUE`, all are finite and positive, and the scan status is `ready`. Bars 0-5 are (open, high, low, close) = (9, 10, 8, 9)u, except bar 1 = (12, 20, 10, 15)u, which gives `n1` = 1u. Bars 6-10 are (C-1, C+1, C-2, C)u for C = 10 to 14, a rise with range 3u. The formation is (15, 18, 14, 17)u, (16, 20, 15, 19)u, (18, 23, 17, 22)u and a strike of (24, 25, 12, 13)u. Every range in the `n2` window (bars 2-11) divides to 0, so `n2` is null. The shipped rule then emits only `black_candle`; with the check deleted it also emits `bullish_three_line_strike`. Overflow, which needs ranges near `Number.MAX_VALUE / 10`, is a second possible route. No test pins this case.
- **The Three Stars `nearRange` null check: equivalent at subnormal prices; overflow not analysed.** Its window (the ten bars before the second candle) also contains the first candle. It can be missing through the same underflow, but only if every range in it is at most 5u. The third candle's short-body window shares nine of those bars, and its tenth bar is the second candle. The first body is then at most 2u, because its lower shadow must exceed it inside a range of at most 5u, and the second body is smaller still. So every body in that window also divides to 0, the third candle is never short, and the rule fires neither with the check nor without it. Whether an overflowing window can tell the two apart was not settled.
- **The rising-shape check inside Rising Three Methods.** Without it, the rule could only also accept the falling colour shape, and that shape needs a white second reaction, which `down(r2)` already forbids.

**Round-4 pins (2026-10-28, same `arx-candle-geometry-v5` rules, no detector change).** A further review showed, with the suite passing, that these could still change. In the shared 3-candle setup, the first candle's mean body could be read one bar later (so the window counts the first candle itself) or one bar earlier, and the prior-trend window could move one bar earlier. The Three-Line Strike strike close could be measured against the first low (bearish: first high) instead of the first open, and the strike open against the third high (bearish: third low) instead of the third close. The Identical Three Crows first lower shadow could use .12 × its mean, or another candle's mean. While adding the trend test, this round also found that moving the 3-candle trend window one bar *later* passed the suite. Each item now has a test, with a passing control beside each near miss:
- **Two Crows, Upside Gap Two Crows, Unique Three-River and Three Stars in the South, one test each:** bar 2, the oldest bar of the first candle's prior-10 window, is widened so the mean body is .1 below the first body (control) and then .1 above it (near miss). - **All ten 3-candle v4/v5 ids, one test each:** prefix closes whose last five bars trend while the five bars one earlier do not (control), and closes whose last five bars show no trend while the window one bar later, which adds the first formation candle, would (near miss).

- **Identical Three Crows:** first lower shadows of .24 (control) and .26 (near miss) against .1 × the first candle's prior-10 mean range (.25). The .26 shadow is below .11 and .12 of that mean (.275, .3) and below .1 of the second and third candles' means (.2696, .2886), so each of those bounds would accept it.
