Previously reported issue: queued delivery depended only on a timer callback,
so an overdue request could be delivered before its delayed timer ran.
Resolved: enqueue now records performance.now()+10000. Receiver registration
checks the deadline before rereading and again immediately before delivery.
The timer remains responsible for displaying a timely unavailable notice.
The new regression moves the monotonic clock beyond expiry without firing the
timer and asserts no delivery. Source review confirms it exercises the defect.

Cancellation and ownership:
- One pending request; a subsequent click replaces and clears the prior timer.
- Pane/source removal and selection changes cancel the pending request.
- Delayed delivery rechecks ownership and compares the reread answer exactly.
- Queue is consumed before receiver invocation, preventing duplicate delivery
  through StrictMode or receiver re-registration.
- Receiver cleanup only clears its own registration; false/throw yields a
  visible failure notice instead of a model/provider fallback.
- Provider cleanup clears outstanding timeout; chart explanation remains local.
